Tagline

Every street has a story.

LORE is a pocket-sized AI travel companion that tells you the stories of the city around you through your own earphones. Quiet luxury, warm travel: Leica’s restraint, Teenage Engineering’s play, Aesop’s warmth, Monocle’s curiosity.

Brand idea · The Keyhole
On the Aventine there’s a keyhole in a green door. Look through it and Rome hands you St Peter’s dome, framed by a hedge. LORE is that keyhole for every street.

Sonic identity

Three notes, like a door opening.

The LORE sonic logo: a rising perfect fifth and a soft octave (D5 → A5 → D6), struck on a felted kalimba with a short brass-bowl tail. 1.2 s. Every earcon is built from these three pitches.

Photo 01 · Golden hour, eye-levelReal light, real people seen from behind or at a distance. Never posed tourists pointing at things.

Photo 02 · Look upCornices, inscriptions, saints on corners. Shoot upward, 35 mm, no tilt correction.

Photo 03 · Through the frameDoorways, arches, keyholes. The story is the thing behind the opening.
