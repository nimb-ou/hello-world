# LORE: naming rationale and trademark notes

**Owner:** Brand (input to Legal-ops) · 2026-10-31
> **This is not legal advice.** Every availability statement below is **UNVERIFIED** and is based on brand-team web searches. Before any filing, launch spend or domain purchase, LORE needs a professional clearance search (full USPTO / EUIPO / WIPO Madrid / UKIPO + common-law search) by trademark counsel.

## 1. Why "LORE"
- **Meaning:** "lore" is *a body of traditions and knowledge on a subject, passed from person to person*. That is exactly the product: the knowledge of a place, passed on by voice.
- **Sound:** one syllable and four letters, easy to say in Italian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Japanese (ロア). There are no known negative meanings in the launch languages, but **native-speaker checks are needed** for the 20 planned cities (e.g. Turkish, Japanese, Catalan).
- **Shape:** L-O-R-E is set in symmetric, stable capitals. The O gives a round counter that rhymes with the Keyhole disc.
- **Brand fit:** "lore" implies stories that are told rather than read, which suits a voice-first device. It is warm and a little mythic, but the "true stories with sources" promise grounds it.
- **Weakness:** "lore" is a **common English word**, so it is weak as a standalone mark and crowded in entertainment and software (see §3). Protection will come from the **combination of LORE + the Keyhole symbol + the specific goods**, and from distinctive feature names.

## 2. Alternates considered

| Name | Idea | Pros | Cons | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cicerone** | Italian for a learned guide who shows visitors around (from Cicero) | Rome-rooted, literally the job | Long, hard to say in English, sounds like a tour company | Backup for a premium **service** name |
| **Strada** | Italian "street" | Warm, simple | Generic and Italy-only. Common in restaurants and cars | No |
| **Murmur** | A voice in your ear | Evocative of audio | Negative ("murmur of complaint"), and a heart condition | No |
| **Plinth** | The base under a monument | Architectural | Cold, and says nothing about stories | No |
| **Wend** | To wander | Short, verb-like | Obscure, and spelling confusion (wind/wend) | No |
| **Oculus** | The Pantheon's eye | Great Rome story | Meta's Oculus VR brand, **high conflict** | No |
| **Keyhole** | The brand idea itself | Strong story | Google's Keyhole (Earth's predecessor) is historic. Security connotations | Use as the **symbol name only** |
| **Lore** | Chosen | See §1 | Crowded word | **Chosen**, with a compound fallback |

**Fallback compounds** (if clearance fails in a key class): **LORE Labs** (company), **Lorewalk**, **Streetlore**, **Lore & Atlas**. Prefer keeping LORE as the logo and adding a descriptor ("LORE Travel") in the registrations.

- Several "Lore" games, AI story apps and crypto/meme tokens exist in the App Store and on the web.
- **No "Lore" consumer hardware or travel device was found** in this search. That is encouraging, but the search was limited.

**Recommended actions (for Legal-ops):**
1. Commission a full clearance search: word mark **LORE** in classes 9, 38, 39, 42 (+ 41, 45), US/EU/UK, plus Madrid for Japan, Turkey and Switzerland.
2. Run a **figurative search for the Keyhole symbol** in 9/42 (keyhole marks are common in security and real estate).
3. If the word mark is crowded, file a **composite (word + Keyhole)** and **LORE LABS** first.
4. Screen and consider filings for feature names: **Map Vision** (likely descriptive and weak, so check), **Walk Me Home** (check safety apps), **City Packs**, **LORE Pass**.
5. Do a native-language connotation check in the top 10 market languages.
