Principle

Three voices, one conversation. Fraunces is the storyteller: the editorial serif for headlines and the moments that should feel like a good magazine. Inter is the local friend: clear and calm in every interface and on every label. JetBrains Mono is the field notebook: coordinates, timestamps, sources and specs.

Fraunces is always set with SOFT 0 · WONK 0. Optical size follows point size. Never use Fraunces below 18 px on screen or 9 pt in print.

Story · Piazza della Rotonda · 28 s

A name on the porch that isn’t the builder’s

Look up at the inscription over the columns: M·AGRIPPA·L·F·COS·TERTIVM·FECIT, “Marcus Agrippa, son of Lucius, consul for the third time, made this.”

Agrippa’s temple burned. The building you’re facing is Hadrian’s rebuild, about AD 118–128, and he put the original builder’s name back on it. Step inside: when it rains, the water falls through the oculus and drains through 22 holes in the floor.

On-device type: Inter 450–500 at opsz 24–32, minimum 18 px (≈ 2.4 mm cap height at ~ 326 ppi, estimate pending Design's panel spec). Fraunces only for one headline per screen at ≥ 30 px. No text on pure black below 60% opacity.

01

Sentence case, always

Headlines and buttons in sentence case. ALL CAPS only for the wordmark and mono overlines.

02

One serif moment

One Fraunces element per view. If everything is editorial, nothing is.

03

Measure 52–68 characters

Body copy never runs wider than 68 ch. Leading 1.5–1.65 for Inter body.

04

Real typography

Curly quotes, true apostrophes, en dashes for ranges (1723–1725), thin spaces in coordinates, tabular figures in data.
