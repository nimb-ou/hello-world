# Children's Data: LORE Position (DRAFT v0.1)

> **DRAFT. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** A privacy lawyer must review this. It covers US COPPA (16 CFR Part 312, amended rule effective 23 Jun 2025, compliance by 22 Apr 2026 [verify]), GDPR Art. 8 (age of digital consent 13–16 by Member State), the UK ICO **Age Appropriate Design Code**, India's DPDP Act s.9 (child = under **18**; verifiable parental consent; no tracking/behavioural monitoring or targeted ads at children), state laws (e.g., California AADC (partially enjoined) [verify], minors' social-media laws), and the EU DSA Art. 28 (if applicable).

## Position
1. **LORE is a general-audience product for adults.** Marketing targets solo travellers aged 22–45 and retirees (COMPANY.md). We **don't direct the Services to children**: no child-oriented content, characters, or ads placed on kids' media.
2. **Accounts are 18+.** Age is declared at sign-up (neutral age gate: date of birth, with no hint about the required age). Under-18 declarations are blocked from creating an account.
3. **Family / supervised mode (roadmap, not v1):** a parent with an adult account may enable a "Family" profile on a device for a teenager **13–17** (India: any under-18 user only with **verifiable parental consent** via DigiLocker-style token or an existing verified adult account per the DPDP Rules [verify]). In that profile: no trip history by default; no voice-recording retention; no marketing; no booking affiliate links; live share only to the parent's contacts; high-privacy defaults (ICO AADC standards 7 and 10).
4. **Under-13 (US) / under the national age of consent (EU):** no accounts. If we learn we hold such data without parental consent, **delete it within 30 days**. Note: a device's location sharing to a parent (a child wearing a parent-account device) is a **parent-controlled use**, so we treat that data with child-level protections but don't create a child profile.
5. **Study-abroad students** (secondary audience) are generally 18+. Programmes involving minors (school trips) go through **LORE for Business** with a DPA in which the school is controller and obtains parental consent.
6. **No targeted advertising, profiling or behavioural monitoring of anyone known to be under 18** (DPDP s.9(3); GDPR Recital 38; state laws).
7. **Audit:** review marketing creative quarterly for child appeal (FTC COPPA "directed to children" factors: subject matter, visual content, animated characters, music, celebrities, ads on kids' sites).

## Product implications
- Age gate in app onboarding and the web store account creation.
- Kickstarter: backers must be 18+ (Kickstarter's own rule), consistent with this.
- Content: stories involving violent history (plagues, executions, wars) are fine for adults; a "Family-friendly narration" filter is a roadmap item.
