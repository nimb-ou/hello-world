## Part A: Your statutory rights come first

**EEA / Switzerland / UK consumers:** in addition to this Limited Warranty, **you have legal rights against the seller** of your LORE product if it isn't in conformity with the contract. In the EU this is **at least 2 years** from delivery (longer in some countries, e.g., **3 years in Portugal**). If a defect appears within **1 year** (2 years in some countries) it's presumed to have existed at delivery. You can choose **repair or replacement**, and in some cases a **price reduction or refund**. **If you choose repair, the legal guarantee period is extended by 12 months** (Right to Repair Directive, as transposed [verify national law]). In the UK, you have a **30-day right to reject**, then repair/replacement, then a final right to reject or a price reduction, with claims possible for up to 6 years (5 in Scotland). **Our Limited Warranty is in addition to, and does not limit, these rights.** Where LORE sold you the product directly, LORE is the seller and handles both.

## Part D: Internal operations notes (not customer-facing)

### D.1 Warranty reserve
- Accrue a **warranty reserve of 3–4% of hardware revenue** in year 1 (new product, new ODM). Review quarterly against actual return/repair data and step down to 2–2.5% once the field failure rate (FFR) is stable below 2%. See `supply-chain-ops.md` §8.
- Track separately: DOA (dead-on-arrival) rate target **<1%**; 12-month FFR target **<3%**; battery capacity claims.
- **ODM cost recovery:** the supply agreement must require the ODM to bear the cost of epidemic failures (>[2]% same root cause), plus parts and labour for defects attributable to manufacturing (`templates/odm-supply-agreement-term-sheet.md`).

### D.2 Return-grading flow
A-grade (as new, opened) → refurbish & resell as "Certified Renewed" · B-grade (cosmetic) → refurb with new housing · C-grade (functional fault) → repair/RMA to ODM · D-grade → harvest parts / recycle via a WEEE-compliant recycler (certificate of destruction; secure data wipe NIST SP 800-88).

### D.3 Lithium-battery returns
Returned devices are **UN3481 Section II** shipments. Damaged/defective batteries are **forbidden on aircraft** (IATA "damaged, defective or recalled" batteries: SP A154) [V], so use **ground-only** labels for suspected swollen units, plus special packaging (P908/LP904 [V]).
