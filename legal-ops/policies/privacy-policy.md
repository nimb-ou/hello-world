## Short version (the "privacy label")

- **Where you are:** LORE uses your location to tell you about what's around you. Most of that happens **on your LORE device**. When we need the cloud (for example, a "tell me more" question), we send only what's needed and **don't keep your location history unless you switch on Trip History.**
- **What you say:** LORE listens **only while you press the button**. We turn your voice into text, answer, and **delete the audio**.
- **Safety:** if you use SOS, live location sharing or "walk me home", we share your location **only with the people you choose**, and only for as long as the feature runs.
- **We don't sell your personal data**, we don't "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we don't put advertising trackers on your device.
- You can **download or delete** your data in the app at any time.

## 3. AI features
LORE uses AI models (on the device and in the cloud) to choose and tell stories, answer questions and generate speech.
- **You are talking to an AI.** LORE narration is AI-generated or AI-assisted and voiced with synthetic speech. We say so in the app and on first use (EU AI Act Art. 50).
- **Fact-grounded:** our story engine is designed to use checked facts with sources. AI can still make mistakes. See the Terms.
- We **don't use your personal data to train general-purpose AI models**. We may use **de-identified, aggregated** signals (e.g., "story X was skipped often") to improve story selection. We use voice recordings for model improvement only with your opt-in consent.
- Our cloud AI providers process requests under contracts that **prohibit them from using your data to train their models** and limit retention to [0–30] days.
- No decision with legal or similarly significant effects is made about you solely by automated means.

## 4. How we share personal data
We share personal data only with:
1. **Service providers (processors)** that help us run the Services: cloud hosting, AI inference, customer support, payments, email, fulfilment/3PL, analytics. Current list: lore.travel/subprocessors. They may use data only on our instructions.
2. **Connectivity partner(s)** that provide the data service in your device: [Partner name]. They receive device and network identifiers and network-level location (cell ID) needed to provide mobile data, and may act as an **independent controller** under telecom law. Their privacy notice: [link].
3. **People you choose:** emergency contacts and live-share recipients.
4. **Emergency relay partner** (where available, e.g., [partner]) if you trigger SOS and choose to have a professional monitoring centre contact emergency services.
5. **Booking partners**, only if you choose to book a hotel, tour or restaurant through LORE. We pass the details needed for the booking, and the partner's own privacy policy applies. We may receive a commission. We don't share your location history with them.
6. **Authorities**, when required by law or to protect someone's life. We'll push back on overbroad requests and tell you where we lawfully can. Transparency report: [link, annually].
7. **Corporate transactions:** a buyer or successor, subject to this policy.

**We do not sell personal data** and do not "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising (CCPA terms). We have not done so in the past 12 months. We honour **Global Privacy Control** signals.

## 7. Your rights
Depending on where you live, you have the right to: **access** your data; **correct** it; **delete** it; **port** it (download in a machine-readable format); **restrict** or **object** to processing (including direct marketing at any time); **withdraw consent** at any time (this doesn't affect earlier processing); **not be subject to solely automated decisions** with significant effects; and to **complain** to a supervisory authority (EU: your local authority, e.g., Italy's Garante; UK: the ICO, after first contacting us, under our complaints process; India: the Data Protection Board after using our grievance process; Japan: the PPC).

**California and other US states:** right to know/access, delete, correct, opt out of sale/sharing/targeted advertising (we don't do these), **limit the use of sensitive personal information**, and appeal a decision (by emailing privacy@lore.travel with "Appeal"). If we deny your appeal, you can contact your state Attorney General. We won't discriminate against you for exercising your rights. Authorised agents may submit requests with proof of authority.

**India:** right to access information, correction, completion, updating, erasure, grievance redressal, and to **nominate** another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.

**How:** in the app (Settings → Privacy → *Download my data* / *Delete*), or email privacy@lore.travel. We'll verify your identity and reply within [1 month (EEA/UK) / 45 days (US) / the DPDP Rules timeframe].

## 8. Children
LORE is **not directed to children under 13** (US) or **under 16** (EEA/UK; or the lower age of digital consent in your country), and accounts require the holder to be **18+** (India: under the DPDP Act, anyone under 18 is a child, so a child may use LORE only with **verifiable parental consent**). A parent or guardian may set up a LORE device for a supervised teenager under their own account. See our Children's Data Approach: [link]. If you think a child has given us personal data without consent, contact us and we'll delete it.
