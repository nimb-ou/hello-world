## 2. Ordering and payment
2.1 We show the total price, billing frequency, renewal date, and how to cancel **before** you subscribe. You confirm with a clearly labelled button ("**Subscribe – pay $9.99/month**").
2.2 We charge your saved payment method at the start of each billing period. In-app purchases via Apple/Google are billed and managed by them under their terms.
2.3 **Failed payments:** we retry for [7] days. Afterwards you go to the Free plan (no data). **SOS to contacts keeps working where technically possible.**
2.4 Taxes: prices include VAT/GST where required. US sales tax is added at checkout where applicable.

## 3. Automatic renewal and reminders
3.1 Explorer renews automatically at the then-current price until you cancel.
3.2 **Reminders:** for annual plans, we email you **[15–45] days before renewal** (California requires annual reminders for annual plans and notices for free-trial conversions [V]). For the free 3-month bundle, we remind you **at least [7] days before** it converts (if you opted into conversion).
3.3 **Free-trial conversion design (recommended):** at device activation we ask: "*Keep Explorer after your 3 free months for $9.99/month?*" **[Yes, auto-renew] / [No, switch me to Free]**. No pre-ticked boxes. This complies with the strictest regimes (CA ARL affirmative consent, EU, UK DMCC, Indian dark-patterns rules).
3.4 **Price changes:** we give **at least 30 days' notice** before a price increase applies. You may cancel before it takes effect. Where the law requires your **express consent** to a price increase (e.g., some EU Member States, and CA ARL for material changes [V]), we'll ask for it, and if you don't give it, your subscription ends at the end of the current period.

## 4. Cancellation
4.1 **Cancel anytime** online, in the app (*Settings → LORE Pass → Cancel*) or via Apple/Google, **in as few steps as it took to sign up**. EU/Germany: a "**Cancel contract**" button is always visible. We don't require a phone call or a chat.
4.2 Cancellation takes effect at the **end of the current billing period**. You keep access until then. Monthly fees aren't refunded for partial months, **except** as required by law or §4.3–4.5.
4.3 **EU/UK 14-day right of withdrawal:** if you subscribe online, you can withdraw within 14 days of the contract date **without giving a reason** using the withdrawal function or the model form. If you asked us to start the service during that period, we refund the full amount **minus a proportionate amount for the service provided** (including data used). If you expressly asked for immediate supply of digital content and acknowledged losing the right, the withdrawal right may be lost for that content.
4.4 **Material reduction of service** (e.g., we drop coverage in a country listed at purchase for the Trip Pass you bought for that trip): you may terminate and get a **pro-rata refund**.
4.5 **Trip Pass:** non-refundable once activated, except under statutory rights or if service is unavailable for [>24 hours] in a covered country (pro-rata refund on request).

## 5. Fair use and network management
5.1 Data is for **personal use on the LORE device**. Tethering/hotspot isn't supported.
5.2 **Permanent roaming:** LORE Pass is designed for travel. If a device uses data mainly in one non-home country for more than [90] consecutive days, our connectivity partner may move it to a local profile, apply local network terms, or limit service, as required by roaming rules (EU Roaming Regulation fair-use rules and carrier restrictions in some countries).
5.3 We may temporarily manage traffic for network integrity, security, or to comply with law (Open Internet Regulation 2015/2120 principles).
5.4 Excessive or abusive use (e.g., automated scraping) may lead to suspension after notice.

## 6. Coverage and service levels

## 7. Transfer of devices
