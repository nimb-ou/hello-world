## 1. Core SOS disclaimer (use everywhere: box, app, website, Kickstarter FAQ)

> **LORE is not an emergency service and does not replace calling emergency services.** In an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in the EU and many countries, 911 in the US/Canada, 999 in the UK, 110/119 in Japan, 112 in India) using a phone if you can.
> LORE's SOS, live location sharing and "walk me home" features send alerts and your location **to the contacts you choose** (and, where offered, to a third-party monitoring service). **They may not work** if the battery is low or empty, there's no mobile, satellite or GNSS signal, the device is damaged, your contacts don't see or respond to the alert, or third-party networks fail. Location may be inaccurate, especially indoors, underground or between tall buildings. **Never rely on LORE as your only means of getting help.**

**Short form (UI / packaging):** "*SOS alerts your chosen contacts. It is not a substitute for calling 112/911.*"

**Atlas satellite SOS (if offered):** "*Satellite messaging needs a clear view of the sky and can take several minutes. Available only in supported countries. It may not connect.*" Name the emergency relay partner and any country limits.

**First-use setup must:** (1) require at least one emergency contact who receives a test message and **confirms**; (2) show the local emergency number for the current country (auto-updated by location); (3) run a **test SOS** flow; (4) explain long-press timing and the cancel window (e.g., 5-second countdown with vibration to prevent false alarms).
