# LORE Acceptable Use Policy (DRAFT v0.1)

> **INTERNAL DRAFT. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** A lawyer must review this before publication. It includes use restrictions that third-party AI model licences require us to pass down to users (Llama Acceptable Use Policy, Gemma Prohibited Use Policy). Re-check those against the model versions we actually ship. [verify]

This AUP is part of the LORE Terms of Service. If you break it, we may suspend or end your access (Terms §7).

## 1. Safety first
- Don't use LORE in a way that puts you or others at risk. For example, don't watch the screen while crossing roads, cycling or driving. LORE is for **pedestrian** use only and is **not** a driving or cycling navigation aid.
- Don't trigger **false SOS alerts** on purpose or make hoax emergency reports. In many countries hoax calls to emergency services are a crime.
- Don't use live location sharing to **track another person without their knowledge and consent**. Hidden placement of a LORE device on someone else, in their bag or on a vehicle is **prohibited stalking**. We cooperate with law enforcement on this and support anti-stalking measures (e.g., unwanted-tracker alerts per the IETF DULT draft standard, where implemented) [verify].

## 2. Lawful and respectful use
Do not use the Services to:
1. Break any law, including privacy, trespass, heritage-site rules, drone and photography restrictions, and local rules on guiding (e.g., some cities require a licence to give paid tours. LORE is for personal use).
2. Harass, threaten, defame, or discriminate against anyone. Don't produce hate speech or sexual content involving minors (zero tolerance; reported to NCMEC/authorities).
3. Photograph or identify people with Atlas "Glance" for surveillance, facial recognition or profiling. Glance is designed for buildings and places.
4. Generate content for: weapons of mass destruction, critical-infrastructure attacks, malware, fraud, disinformation campaigns, election manipulation, or any use prohibited by the **Llama Acceptable Use Policy** or the **Gemma Prohibited Use Policy** (incorporated by reference: [links]).
5. Use LORE content to train competing AI models or build a competing story database, or to scrape, bulk-download or resell stories, audio or City Packs (except open-licence data under its licence).
6. Remove attribution notices (OpenStreetMap, Wikipedia, licensors).

## 3. Device, network and security
- Don't tamper with the eSIM, try to move the data plan to another device, tether, or resell connectivity.
- Don't attempt unauthorised access, probe or attack our systems (good-faith security research under our **Vulnerability Disclosure Policy** at lore.travel/security is welcome and protected by its safe harbour).
- Don't circumvent subscription, region or security controls.

## 4. Commercial use
Using LORE for paid tours, rentals or fleets needs a **LORE for Business** agreement.

## 5. Reporting
Report abuse, misuse of tracking or harmful content: abuse@lore.travel or in the app. Urgent risk to life: contact local emergency services first.
