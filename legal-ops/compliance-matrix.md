**Low-value import regimes (for direct-to-consumer shipping from a hub):**
- **US:** **de minimis ($800) is suspended for all countries** since 29 Aug 2025 (EO 14324), continued after the IEEPA ruling (EO of 20 Feb 2026); CBP interim final rules of **24 Jun 2026** codify it for postal and other modes [S]. **Plan to import in bulk (formal entry) into a US 3PL**, paying duty once, rather than shipping DTC from Asia.
- **EU:** VAT due on all imports since July 2021; **IOSS** for consignments ≤€150 lets us collect VAT at checkout. The EU agreed to **remove the €150 customs-duty exemption** and introduce a flat per-item handling duty (target date 2026) [V, verify current status]. Prefer **bulk import into an EU 3PL** (Netherlands/Germany) with an **EORI number** and **fiscal representative** / indirect customs representative. Then sales are EU-domestic B2C, with VAT via **OSS (Union scheme)**.
- **UK:** ≤£135: VAT collected at point of sale by the seller (register for UK VAT). Bulk import into a UK 3PL is preferable; UK EORI.
- **India:** courier imports have limits; B2C import of devices is complex (BIS/WPC). **Sell in India only via an Indian importer/distributor** (later phase).
- **Japan:** ¥10,000 duty/tax-free threshold for small consignments [V]; use a Japanese importer of record.

**Rules of origin / tariff engineering:** final assembly + test outside China (Vietnam, Malaysia, Thailand, India under PLI) can change origin if it gives a **substantial transformation** (US test) or meets the EU non-preferential origin rules. Don't "transship". CBP enforces aggressively (EAPA). Document real manufacturing steps.

**Export controls:** encryption in BLE/TLS/LTE. Mass-market items are generally **EAR99 or 5A992.c** under **License Exception ENC §740.17(b)(1)** with **self-classification** reporting (annual self-classification report to BIS/NSA, due Feb 1) [V]. Satellite NTN and camera don't change this materially. Screen customers/partners against OFAC/BIS lists in the store checkout (Shopify/Stripe Radar plus a restricted-country block: Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Crimea/DNR/LNR regions).

## 5. eSIM, telecom and satellite regulatory

### 5.1 Commercial structure (recommended)
**LORE does not become a licensed operator.** We contract with a **connectivity provider of record** (an MVNO/MVNE with global roaming + local profiles + an eSIM platform). The partner holds operator licences, lawful-intercept, number/IMSI resources and carrier contracts. LORE is a **brand reseller / bundler** of data inside LORE Pass. Candidates (non-exhaustive; diligence required): **1GLOBAL (incl. ex-Truphone)**, **Eseye**, **emnify**, **floLIVE**, **Kigen (eIM/SGP.32 platform)**, **Transatel (NTT)**, **Telna**, **Monogoto**, **Airalo (B2B/partner)**, **Skylo** (satellite NTN for Atlas).

> **EU Data Act (Regulation (EU) 2023/2854)** [V]: LORE devices are "connected products". Micro/small enterprises are exempt from some data-holder obligations. **Build a "Download my device data" export** into the app (also needed for GDPR portability).
