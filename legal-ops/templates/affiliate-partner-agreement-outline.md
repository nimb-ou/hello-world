# Affiliate / Booking Partner Agreement: Outline

> **OUTLINE. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** A lawyer must turn this into a full agreement. It covers LORE earning commissions on hotels, tours, restaurants and experiences booked via the device/app. Most large partners (Booking.com, Expedia (EPS/Affiliate), GetYourGuide, Viator, TheFork, Klook, Tiqets, Musement) use **their own standard affiliate terms**, so this outline doubles as a **review checklist** for those terms and as the template for direct local partners (e.g., a Rome trattoria, a small tour operator).

## 1. Parties and scope
- LORE Labs, Inc. (Affiliate/Publisher) and [Partner] (Merchant/Supplier).
- Channels: LORE device voice ("Want me to book a table?"), app, website, emails. **Voice/AI recommendation channels are often not anticipated in partner terms, so get express permission.**

## 2. Commercials
| Item | Typical range (market estimates; verify with each partner) |
|---|---|
| Hotels (OTA affiliate) | 25–40% of the OTA's commission (≈ 4–6% of booking value) |
| Tours/activities (GYG, Viator) | ~8% of booking value [verify] |
| Restaurants (direct) | €[1–3] per seated cover, or a flat referral fee |
| Attribution | Last-click, [30]-day cookie / deep-link token; app/device referrals via partner API with a LORE affiliate ID |
| Payment | Monthly, net 30–60 after stay/experience completion; cancellations clawed back |
| Reporting | Dashboard/API access; audit right 1×/yr |

## 3. LORE obligations
- Brand-use guidelines; no trademark bidding on partner brands in paid search without consent.
- Accurate content (prices/availability from the API, not hard-coded); **real-time availability** where shown.
- **Disclosures:** label results "Partner" / "LORE earns a commission". FTC Endorsement Guides; EU UCPD and Omnibus Directive (**ranking transparency**: explain main ranking parameters and paid placement); UK DMCC; Indian dark-patterns rules. **Editorial independence:** stories (historical narration) are never paid placements; partner suggestions are clearly separated ("Nearby: a partner trattoria…").
- No incentivised fake reviews.

## 4. Partner obligations
- Honour bookings; customer service for the booked service (the partner is the contracting party for the service).
- Supply accurate data/APIs with SLA; notify of closures.
- Comply with consumer law, accessibility, health and safety, and tourism licensing.
- Insurance (for tour operators: public liability ≥ €[2M]).

## 5. Data protection
- Each party is an **independent controller** for booking data (LORE passes only the data needed: name, contact, booking details). **No location history shared.**
- Data-sharing agreement / SCCs for transfers; no partner retargeting of LORE users without consent; no use of LORE user data to build profiles.

## 6. Liability
- LORE isn't liable for the partner's services. The partner indemnifies LORE for claims arising from its services (injury on a tour, food poisoning, overbooking).
- Package Travel Directive (EU) 2015/2302 risk: **don't bundle** a hotel + tour in a way that creates a "package" or "linked travel arrangement" with LORE as organiser. Keep bookings separate, each with the partner as the contracting party (counsel to review flows).

## 7. Term and termination
12 months, auto-renew; 30 days' convenience termination; immediate for breach or brand harm; commissions earned before termination still paid.

## 8. Checklist when reviewing big-partner affiliate terms
- [ ] Are **voice/AI assistants and devices** permitted channels?
- [ ] Is caching/offline display of listings allowed (offline City Packs)? Usually not, so show partner results only when online.
- [ ] Brand/logo display rules on a 1.6" AMOLED.
- [ ] Commission on cancellations / non-refundable rates.
- [ ] Payment thresholds and currency; tax forms.
- [ ] Unilateral changes to commission rates (notice period?).
- [ ] Exclusivity or MFN demands: reject.
- [ ] Governing law/forum.
