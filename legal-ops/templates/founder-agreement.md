## Part A: Founders' Agreement

**Parties:** LORE Labs, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and the founders listed in Schedule 1 (each a "Founder").
**Effective date:** [date]

1. **Purpose.** The Founders will build LORE, a pocket-sized AI travel companion (devices, software, content and subscription services), as described in the Company's operating plan.
2. **Roles and time commitment.**

   | Founder | Title | Responsibilities | Commitment |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Nimit Jain | CEO, Director | Product vision, fundraising, hiring, final say on strategy | Full-time from [date] |
   | [Co-founder] | [CTO / COO] | [Engineering / hardware / operations] | Full-time from [date] |

   Each Founder devotes substantially all working time to the Company. Outside activities need **board approval** if they compete, use Company resources, or take more than [5] hours/week.
3. **Equity.** Schedule 1 sets out each Founder's shares, all subject to vesting under the RSPA (Part B). No Founder has any promise of further equity except by board action.
4. **Decision-making.** Ordinary decisions by the CEO. "Major decisions" (fundraising terms, issuing equity, budgets >$[50k], hiring C-level, IP licensing outside the ordinary course, entering debt, changing core product direction, selling the company) need **board approval** (and, until the board has an independent member, unanimous Founder consent for [selling the company / removing a founder director]).
5. **Intellectual property.** Each Founder **assigns to the Company all right, title and interest** in LORE-related IP created before or after incorporation (code, designs, Map Vision algorithms, story-engine designs, City Pack data, brand, domain names including lore.travel, social handles) by the **Technology Assignment Agreement** and **CIIAA** signed with this agreement. Each Founder lists excluded **prior inventions** in Schedule 2, and confirms that no former employer or university has rights in the assigned IP. **Risk:** if a founder created LORE work while employed elsewhere (especially in California or under a broad invention-assignment clause), counsel must review that employer agreement.
6. **Confidentiality and non-solicitation.** Per the CIIAA. Non-competes are not used (unenforceable in California and restricted elsewhere). Non-solicitation of employees for 12 months after departure where lawful.
7. **Compensation.** Founder salaries are set by the board. Until the pre-seed closes: [$0 / deferred]. Proposed post-pre-seed: [$90k–$120k] (see `../hr-hiring.md`).
8. **Departure.**
   - **Voluntary resignation / termination for Cause:** unvested shares repurchased at the **lower of cost and FMV**. Vested shares kept, subject to ROFR.
   - **Termination without Cause / resignation for Good Reason:** unvested shares repurchased at cost, **except** double-trigger acceleration on change of control (Part B).
   - Death/disability: [6/12] months' additional vesting acceleration (optional).
   - The departing Founder resigns all director/officer positions and returns materials.
9. **Deadlock.** Escalate to a mediator chosen by the board (e.g., the lead investor's partner or an independent advisor). If unresolved in 30 days, the CEO decides operational matters. Board matters follow the bylaws.
10. **Founder representations.** No conflicting agreements; no litigation; accurate prior-invention list; compliance with immigration/work authorisation and foreign-investment rules where they live.
11. **Governing law:** Delaware. **Disputes:** confidential arbitration (JAMS, [Wilmington/SF]), with injunctive relief available in court.
12. **Entire agreement and amendments:** with the RSPA, CIIAA and Technology Assignment; amendment by written consent of the Company and all Founders.

## Part B: RSPA key terms (for counsel)

| Term | Proposal |
|---|---|
| Security | Common Stock, par $0.00001 |
| Price | Par value = FMV at incorporation (no assets) |
| Vesting | 4 years; 25% at 1-year cliff; 1/48 monthly thereafter; continuous service as employee, director or consultant |
| Repurchase option | Company may repurchase unvested shares within 90 days of termination, at the lower of cost or FMV |
| Acceleration | **Double-trigger:** 100% of unvested shares if terminated without Cause / for Good Reason within 3 months before or 12 months after a Change of Control |
| Definitions | "Cause" (fraud, felony, material breach of CIIAA, wilful misconduct after a cure notice); "Good Reason" (material pay cut, relocation >50 miles, material reduction in duties, after notice and a cure period) |
| Transfer restrictions | No transfers of unvested shares; ROFR in the bylaws; standard lock-up (180 days at IPO) |
| Escrow | Unvested shares held by the Secretary with a stock power |
| **83(b)** | Founder **must** file within 30 days (Form 15620 online or by certified mail) and give the Company a copy. Company provides the form |
| Spousal consent | Required in community-property states (CA, TX, WA, etc.) |
| Tax | Founder acknowledges reliance on own tax advisers (US and home country) |
| QSBS | Company will use reasonable efforts to keep QSBS records (gross assets, active business) |
