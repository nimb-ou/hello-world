# Offer Letter (TEMPLATE: US at-will employee; EU/India via EOR addendum)

> **TEMPLATE. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Employment counsel must review this. US states require various notices (e.g., California pay-transparency disclosure of the pay scale on request and in postings (SB 1162), New York and Washington pay ranges; Wage Theft Prevention Act notices in CA/NY). For employees outside the US hired via an **EOR (Deel/Remote)**, the EOR issues a local employment contract and this letter becomes a "**Company offer summary + equity side letter**". Options need board approval and a current **409A**.

### EOR addendum (non-US hires)
- Legal employer: [Deel/Remote local entity]; local contract governs salary (in local currency: **[€/₹/£] [amount]**), notice periods, statutory benefits, probation (e.g., Portugal 90/180 days; India 3–6 months; Germany ≤6 months).
- LORE grants equity directly (options under the EIP with a local sub-plan or addendum, e.g., UK EMI isn't available for EOR employees of a non-UK company without a UK PE [verify]; India: options taxed as perquisites at exercise; EU: country-specific tax).
- IP assignment: an EOR contract assigns IP to the EOR, which must **assign it on to LORE**. Confirm the EOR's IP-flow-through clause **and** have the employee sign the LORE CIIAA directly.
