# Confidential Information and Invention Assignment Agreement (CIIAA): TEMPLATE

> **TEMPLATE. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Employment counsel must localise this per country. Notes: **California Labor Code §2870** carve-out (notice mandatory; also required notices in WA, IL, MN, NC, KS, DE, UT, NV, NJ and others); **DTSA §1833(b) whistleblower immunity notice** (required for exemplary damages); **NLRA §7** rights (no overbroad confidentiality restricting discussion of wages/conditions for non-supervisory US employees); **EU/UK**: employee inventions may vest in the employer by statute (UK Patents Act 1977 s.39; Germany's **ArbnErfG** requires inventor notification and **statutory compensation**), moral rights cannot be fully waived in many EU states; **India**: assignment must be express. Copyright Act 1957 s.17 vests work in the employer for works made in the course of employment, but a written assignment is still recommended. Use **contractor IP terms** for non-employees (see `contractor-agreement.md`).

**Between:** LORE Labs, Inc. (the "Company") and [Employee name] ("I").
**In consideration of** my employment, compensation and access to Confidential Information, I agree:

## 1. Confidential Information
1.1 "**Confidential Information**" means non-public information about the Company, its products, technology (including Map Vision algorithms, sensor-fusion parameters, story-engine prompts, models, evaluation data, City Pack data pipelines, firmware, hardware designs, BOMs, supplier terms), business plans, financials, customers, users (including personal data), investors and employees, and third-party information the Company holds in confidence.
1.2 I'll use it only for my work, protect it with reasonable care, and not disclose it except as authorised, both during employment and afterwards (trade secrets: as long as they stay trade secrets; other information: [5] years after employment ends).
1.3 **Exclusions:** information that's public through no fault of mine, that I knew lawfully before (documented), or that I independently developed without using Confidential Information.
1.4 **Permitted disclosures:** nothing here stops me from (a) reporting possible violations of law to a government agency, regulator or a court, or making other disclosures protected by whistleblower laws (including the EU Whistleblower Directive 2019/1937); (b) discussing wages, hours and working conditions as protected by law (US NLRA §7); (c) disclosing as required by law, after notice to the Company where lawful.
1.5 **DTSA notice:** under 18 U.S.C. §1833(b), I won't be held criminally or civilly liable under any trade-secret law for disclosing a trade secret (i) in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely to report or investigate a suspected violation of law, or (ii) in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit.
1.6 **Personal data:** I'll follow the Company's privacy and security policies, access user data only as needed for my role, and never export location data or voice data outside approved systems.

## 2. Inventions
2.1 "**Inventions**" means inventions, discoveries, designs, developments, software, models, datasets, prompts, works of authorship, trade secrets and improvements, whether or not patentable, that I make alone or with others during my employment.
2.2 **Assignment.** I **hereby assign** (and agree to assign) to the Company all right, title and interest in all Inventions that (a) relate to the Company's actual or anticipated business, R&D, or (b) result from work I perform for the Company, or (c) use Company equipment, supplies, facilities, data or Confidential Information ("**Company Inventions**"). Copyrightable works are "works made for hire" to the extent permitted by law.
2.3 **Excluded (California Labor Code §2870 and equivalents):** this assignment does not apply to an Invention I developed entirely on my own time without using Company equipment, supplies, facilities or trade secrets, unless it (i) relates to the Company's business or actual or demonstrably anticipated R&D, or (ii) results from work I performed for the Company. *(Exhibit B reproduces §2870 in full.)*
2.4 **Prior Inventions.** Exhibit A lists my prior inventions that I own (or have an interest in) and that I exclude from assignment. If I incorporate a Prior Invention into Company work, I grant the Company a **non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable, perpetual, worldwide licence** (with the right to sublicense) to use and commercialise it.
2.5 **Disclosure and records.** I'll promptly disclose Company Inventions (e.g., via an Invention Disclosure Form), keep records (Git/Notion), and **not publicly disclose Inventions** (talks, papers, blogs, open-source pushes, social media) without prior written approval from Legal (patent novelty).
2.6 **Further assurances.** I'll sign documents needed to secure the Company's rights (patent filings, assignments). I appoint the Company as my attorney-in-fact for this purpose if I'm unavailable.
2.7 **Moral rights.** To the extent permitted by law, I waive (or agree not to assert) moral rights in Company Inventions.
2.8 **Statutory employee-inventor compensation** (e.g., Germany ArbnErfG; UK Patents Act s.40) is unaffected where mandatory.
2.9 **Open-source contributions** to third-party projects need Legal approval (policy in `../ip-strategy.md` §5.5).
2.10 **AI tools.** I'll use only Company-approved AI tools (enterprise terms with no training on inputs) for Company work, and won't paste Confidential Information, source code or user data into unapproved tools.

## 3. No conflicts
I won't bring or use any former employer's or third party's confidential information. I'm not bound by any agreement that conflicts with this one, except as listed in Exhibit A. During employment I won't engage in competing business or assist competitors.

## 4. Return of materials
On leaving (or on request), I'll return all Company property and data and delete copies from personal devices and accounts, and certify this in writing.

## 5. Non-solicitation (where lawful)
For 12 months after my employment ends, I won't solicit Company employees or contractors to leave. **No customer non-solicit or non-compete** for California employees (B&P Code §16600 et seq.) or where prohibited.

## 6. General
At-will employment is not changed (US). Delaware law (or the mandatory law of my work location). Injunctive relief available. Severability; survival; entire agreement on this subject; amendments in writing.
