# Advisor Agreement (FAST-style) TEMPLATE

> **TEMPLATE. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Based on the structure of the Founder Institute's free **FAST (Founder/Advisor Standard Template)** (fi.co/fast). Download the current official version and compare before use, because FI updates it and warns that modified versions should be checked against the original. Equity grants need **board approval** and must be made under the Equity Incentive Plan (options as NSOs, or restricted stock awards) at an exercise price ≥ FMV (409A) to avoid §409A problems. Securities-law exemption: **Rule 701** (US). For non-US advisors, check local securities/tax rules.

**Between:** LORE Labs, Inc. ("Company") and [Advisor name] ("Advisor"). **Date:** [ ]

## 1. Services
Advisor will provide advice and help, as reasonably requested, in [area: e.g., hardware supply chain in Shenzhen / consumer electronics retail / travel industry partnerships / GNSS-IMU sensor fusion / AI safety and evaluation / EU regulatory]. Expected commitment per the engagement level below. Advisor may be named publicly as an advisor (website, deck) with consent.

## 2. Engagement level and equity (FAST-style grid)
The official FAST table sets equity by **company stage** and **advisor engagement**. For example, the FI's published table lists **0.25% (Idea) / 0.20% (Startup) / 0.15% (Growth)** for a "Standard" advisor with monthly meetings, rising for "Strategic" and "Expert" levels ([FI FAST](https://fi.co/fast)). LORE is at the **"Startup" stage** (pre-seed raised, prototype).

| Level | Commitment | LORE proposed grant (% of fully diluted at grant) |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Monthly 1-hour call; email availability | **0.20%** |
| Strategic | Standard + recruiting help, intros, 2–4 hrs/month | **0.40%** |
| Expert / "Advisor-in-residence" | Strategic + project work 1 day/month (e.g., ODM selection, certification strategy) | **0.60–1.00%** |

## 3. Vesting
**Monthly over [24] months**, with a **3-month cliff** (FAST convention, so an unproductive advisor can part ways before anything vests), subject to continued service. **Acceleration:** 100% of unvested on a Change of Control (single trigger is common for advisors). NSO post-termination exercise window: [12 months] (more advisor-friendly than 90 days).

## 4. Confidentiality and IP
Advisor keeps Company information confidential (Mutual NDA terms incorporated). Ideas and work product Advisor contributes **in the scope of the services** are assigned to the Company. Advisor's general know-how and own prior IP are excluded. Advisor will flag conflicts with other engagements (other advised companies listed in Schedule 1).

## 5. Expenses and cash
No cash compensation unless agreed in writing. Pre-approved expenses reimbursed.

## 6. Term and termination
Either party may end the engagement at any time on written notice. Vesting stops on termination.

## 7. Independent status
Advisor is not an employee and can't bind the Company. Advisor is responsible for own taxes.

## 8. No conflicting obligations; compliance
Advisor confirms that providing advice doesn't breach duties to an employer (e.g., an advisor employed by Apple, Google, Garmin or a carrier must check their employer's policies).
