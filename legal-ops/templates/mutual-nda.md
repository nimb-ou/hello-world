# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (TEMPLATE)

> **TEMPLATE. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** A lawyer must review this before use. Use it with ODMs, component suppliers, eSIM/connectivity partners, content partners and potential B2B customers. **Investors usually won't sign NDAs. Don't ask them;** share only non-enabling material instead. For Chinese ODMs, an **NNN agreement (Non-use, Non-disclosure, Non-circumvention), governed by PRC law, in Chinese, enforceable in a Chinese court** is far more effective than a US NDA. See the note at the end.

**This Mutual NDA** is entered into on [date] between **LORE Labs, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation, [address] ("LORE"), and **[Counterparty]**, [entity type, jurisdiction, address] ("Counterparty"). Each is a "Discloser" when sharing and a "Recipient" when receiving.

1. **Purpose.** To evaluate and discuss a potential business relationship regarding [the design, manufacture and supply of LORE devices / connectivity services / content licensing] (the "**Purpose**").
2. **Confidential Information.** All non-public business, technical, financial, product, user or supplier information disclosed in any form, marked or reasonably understood to be confidential, including designs, CAD, schematics, firmware, BOMs, pricing, roadmaps, algorithms and samples, plus the existence and terms of the discussions.
3. **Obligations.** The Recipient will (a) use Confidential Information **only for the Purpose**; (b) not disclose it except to its employees, officers, advisers and approved subcontractors who need to know and are bound by written obligations at least as protective (the Recipient is liable for them); (c) protect it with at least reasonable care; (d) **not reverse-engineer, decompile or analyse** samples or prototypes, except as needed for the Purpose with written consent; (e) not file patent or design applications based on the other party's Confidential Information.
4. **Exclusions.** Information that is or becomes public without breach; was lawfully known to the Recipient before disclosure (documented); is received lawfully from a third party without a duty of confidence; or is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information.
5. **Compelled disclosure.** Permitted where required by law, with prompt prior notice (if lawful) and cooperation to seek protection.
6. **Term.** The agreement covers disclosures for [2] years from the effective date. Obligations last [5] years from each disclosure. **Trade secrets and source code: for as long as they remain trade secrets.**
7. **Return/destruction** on request or termination, with written certification (backup copies under routine retention stay protected).
8. **No licence; no obligation.** No IP rights are granted. No obligation to proceed. Information is provided "as is".
9. **Non-solicitation (optional):** neither party will solicit the other's employees involved in the Purpose for 12 months (general advertisements excepted).
10. **Personal data.** If personal data is exchanged, the parties will sign a data-protection agreement first. None is expected under this NDA.
11. **Export control.** Each party complies with applicable export laws.
12. **Remedies.** Breach may cause irreparable harm; injunctive relief available without bond, in addition to damages.
13. **Governing law and forum:** [Delaware law; courts of Delaware] / [for Asian suppliers: arbitration under HKIAC or SIAC rules, seated in Hong Kong/Singapore, in English].
14. **General.** Entire agreement on this subject; amendments in writing; no assignment without consent (except to a successor in a merger); counterparts and e-signatures valid.

Signed for LORE Labs, Inc.: ________ Name/Title/Date
Signed for [Counterparty]: ________ Name/Title/Date

---
**China NNN note:** for Shenzhen/Dongguan ODMs and mould makers, use a bilingual NNN contract with: PRC governing law; jurisdiction of the supplier's local People's Court; **liquidated damages** in a fixed RMB amount (Chinese courts readily enforce these); a clause that the supplier must not make, sell or let others make products using LORE's designs or moulds; and **company chop** execution. Register LORE trademarks in China **before** sharing designs (see `../ip-strategy.md`). Budget ~$1.5k–$3k for a China-qualified lawyer to prepare it.
