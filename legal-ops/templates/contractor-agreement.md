# Independent Contractor / Consulting Agreement (TEMPLATE)

> **TEMPLATE. NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** A lawyer must review this before use. **Misclassification risk:** the US (California **ABC test**, AB 5; DOL rule), the UK (IR35 off-payroll rules for medium/large clients; LORE is "small" for now), the EU (e.g., Spain's and Portugal's presumptions, the Platform Work Directive) and India (SEZ/labour codes) can treat long-term, full-time, controlled contractors as employees, with back taxes and benefits owed. Contractors working >6 months full-time on core work should move to employment/EOR (see `../hr-hiring.md`). Also watch **permanent-establishment** risk for the Company abroad.

**Between:** LORE Labs, Inc. ("Company") and [Name / entity], [address, country] ("Contractor").
**Effective date:** [date]

1. **Services.** Contractor will perform the services in each **Statement of Work (SOW)** (template below). Contractor controls the manner, means, hours and location of work, supplies its own equipment, and may work for others (but not on competing location-based travel narration devices during the term without consent).
2. **Fees and payment.** Per SOW: [hourly/fixed/milestone]. Invoices monthly; payment **net 15** via [Deel / Wise / bank]. Contractor bears its own taxes, social contributions and insurance. US contractors provide a **W-9** (Company issues a 1099-NEC). Non-US contractors provide a **W-8BEN/W-8BEN-E**. Expenses only if pre-approved in writing.
3. **Independent contractor status.** No employment, partnership or agency. No benefits. Contractor can't bind the Company.
4. **Intellectual property.**
   4.1 **Assignment:** Contractor **hereby assigns** to the Company all right, title and interest in all deliverables, work product, inventions, code, designs, data, content and documentation created in performing the Services ("**Work Product**"), on creation and upon payment of the fees owed [*note: some lawyers prefer assignment regardless of payment; negotiate*]. Copyright works are works made for hire where permitted.
   4.2 **Moral rights** waived/not asserted to the extent permitted.
   4.3 **Background IP:** Contractor keeps pre-existing tools. If incorporated into Work Product, Contractor grants a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence (with the right to sublicense) to use them as part of the Work Product.
   4.4 **Third-party and open-source components:** only with prior written approval and listed in the SOW (licence policy: `../ip-strategy.md` §5.5). **No GPL/AGPL** without approval. **No AI-generated code or content from unapproved tools**, and disclose AI tool usage.
   4.5 **Further assurances**; power of attorney for filings.
   4.6 **India-based contractors:** the assignment must specify duration and territory (Copyright Act s.19). State "perpetual and worldwide" expressly, because s.19(5)/(6) default to 5 years / India only if they're unspecified.
5. **Confidentiality.** As in the Company's Mutual NDA, plus the DTSA whistleblower notice. Return/delete materials on termination and certify.
6. **Data protection.** If Contractor accesses personal data: process only on Company instructions, keep it secure, report breaches within 24 hours, use no sub-processors without consent. Plus a GDPR Art. 28 addendum where applicable.
7. **Warranties.** Original work; no infringement; no conflicting obligations; compliance with laws (including anti-bribery: FCPA / UK Bribery Act; sanctions).
8. **Indemnity.** Contractor indemnifies the Company for third-party IP infringement claims caused by Contractor's Work Product (capped at [2× fees paid / $x], except for wilful misconduct).
9. **Limitation of liability.** Neither party is liable for indirect damages. Each party's cap = fees paid/payable in the prior 12 months, except breaches of confidentiality, IP assignment, data protection and indemnity.
10. **Term and termination.** Until terminated. Either party may terminate on [14] days' written notice, or immediately for material breach. The Company pays for work accepted to the termination date. Sections 4–9 and 11 survive.
11. **Non-solicitation:** 12 months, employees only (where lawful).
12. **Insurance** (for hardware/test-lab contractors): professional liability ≥ $[1M].
13. **Governing law:** Delaware / [the Contractor's local law where mandatory]. Disputes: [arbitration / courts].
14. **General:** entire agreement; SOW precedence for commercial terms; assignment by the Company to affiliates/successors; notices by email.
