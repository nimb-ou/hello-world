# LORE: Every street has a story.

**LORE Labs, Inc.** (Delaware C-corp, planned) · Founder & CEO: Nimit Jain (nimitjain.248@gmail.com) · Pre-seed, November 2026 · Confidential

**The ask: $1.75M pre-seed SAFE at a $12M post-money cap** ($1.5M plus a $250k top-up, decision D14). Seed of $6M planned after Kickstarter (Aug 2027).

## What it is
A pocket-sized AI travel companion that tells you the true, sourced stories of the city around you through your own earphones. Online or offline, with the phone left in your pocket. It covers history, hidden facts, directions, places to eat and stay, and safety features for solo travellers.

## Problem
In a new city the phone does everything at once: guide, map, wallet and lifeline. It runs out of battery by late afternoon, it makes you a target (Greater Rome had 33,455 pickpocketing incidents in 2024, +68% vs 2019), roaming still causes bill shock (>70% of frequent travellers in one survey), and reading Wikipedia in front of a monument means you miss what is in front of you.

## Product
- **Map Vision:** L1+L5 GNSS, a 9-axis IMU, step direction and building outlines tell LORE what is on your **left, right, ahead, behind** or worth a **"look up"**. There is no camera on the first models.
- **Verified stories:** 20–40 s stories built only from fact cards that each carry a source. The AI narrates but cannot invent history (D4).
- **Why a device:** built-in global eSIM (Square/Atlas), offline City Packs, a 2+ day battery separate from the phone, physical buttons (talk, "what's around me", SOS).

## Traction to date (honest)
Pre-revenue, with no customers yet. A **working Rome prototype** (phone PWA) runs the real story engine on a **Rome City Pack of 86 places and 215 sourced fact cards**. In a **simulated** 1.93 km walk it made 17 narrations, repeated no facts, met 100% of quiet moments (crossings and conversations) and had a mean heading error of 4.3°. 61 + 16 automated tests pass. Hardware architecture, costed BOMs and simulations exist for all three models. Gate G0 user tests start now.

## Market
TAM is **$10.7B/yr** top-down (2027) and **$24.0B** bottom-up. SAM is **8.7M travellers ≈ $1.44B/yr**. Research's 2030 SOM is **$93M** (250k units). There are tailwinds: 1.52B international arrivals in 2025, and travel eSIM is growing from $1.8B to $8.7B (2025 to 2030).

## Competition
Google/Samsung Gemini glasses, Meta Ray-Ban, and "phone + AI-guide app + eSIM" are the main threats. Nobody else combines verified, directional stories with offline packs, its own data and SOS in a standalone, eyes-up device. Humane and Rabbit failed as general assistants with no daily job. Plaud shows that one narrow job, a clip-on device and a subscription can work.
