LORE LABS
Company HQ · updated 31 Oct 2026, evening

Every street has a story.

LORE is a pocket-sized travel companion that tells you the stories of the city around you through your own earphones: history, hidden facts, live info and directions, with "on your left / on your right" awareness. It works online and offline. This page gathers the whole company in one place: the product, the prototype, the numbers, and what each department has produced.

Interface

What you see and tap

The device shows a compass, the story playing and which side it is on, places around you, and the SOS flow. The phone app handles setup, City Packs, the day's recap, safety contacts and the subscription.

Needs the founder

Five things that need you next

Before incorporating

Indian legal opinion

You live in India, so Indian rules on owning a foreign company apply. Book an Indian lawyer for a FEMA/ODI and tax opinion before forming the US company. Budget $600–2,500.

By Dec 2026

Commission the name search

You chose to keep LORE. Order a trademark clearance search ($1.5–4k). "LORE TRAVEL" is the fallback if it fails.

Before buying a domain

Pick a web domain

Your email is now on the deck and one-pager. Check whether lore.travel or a close alternative is free before the website goes live.

Before any walk test

Fact-check the stories

Verify the Mumbai and Rome facts against their sources, then do an editorial pass. A local historian or heritage-walk guide is the ideal reviewer.

At DVT, mid 2027

Square's cost

The biggest swing in the plan. If landed cost stays near $227 instead of falling to about $185, the company needs about $19M more. The fallback is pricing Square at $329.

Operating plan

Four gates before scale

50 prototype units. 1.5-day battery, left/right right 90%+ of the time in the real city.

Jun → Aug 2027

G2 · Demand

Kickstarter: $1M+ pledged from 3,000+ backers at under $60 per backer.

Sep 2027 → Apr 2028

First 5,000 units. Returns under 5%, subscription take-up over 55%.
