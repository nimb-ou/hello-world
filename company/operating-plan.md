# LORE Labs — 18-Month Operating Plan (Nov 2026 → Apr 2028)

The company's job is to remove the biggest risk at each stage before it spends money on the next one. Each stage ends in a **gate**: a test we must pass before moving on.

## Stage 0: Prove the magic (Nov 2026 – Jan 2027)
**Risk to remove:** do people *love* walking with LORE's stories?
- Ship the **phone prototype (PWA)**, the **Mumbai test pack** (founder walk tests, D19) and the **Rome City Pack v1**, with 300+ places checked by hand.
- Run 20 walking tests in Mumbai (Fort/Kala Ghoda) plus 200 remote users in Rome simulation mode; 5 tests in Rome before Kickstarter.
- Run 20 user interviews and a survey with at least 500 responses (see `research/customer-survey.md`).
- Build a waitlist landing page; target 5,000 sign-ups.
- **Gate G0** (all must be true):
  - **≥40%** of testers say they'd be "very disappointed" without LORE (the Sean Ellis test)
  - **≥60%** finish a 30-minute walk
  - **≥25%** of survey respondents say they'd pay $149 or more for a device

## Stage 1: Prove the device (Feb – May 2027)
**Risk to remove:** can a small device beat the phone?
- Build 50 hand-built **EVT** units (dev-board prototype in a 3D-printed or CNC-machined case). Software runs offline on the device.
- Write the requirements for the contract manufacturer, ask 3 contract manufacturers in Vietnam/Malaysia for quotes (D13), and lock the main chip (SoC) choice.
- Raise the pre-seed: **$1.5M** SAFE at a **$12M** post-money valuation cap.
- Hire the first team members: firmware lead, ML/voice engineer, industrial designer (contract), content lead.
- **Gate G1:**
  - EVT battery lasts **≥1.5 days** on the measured usage profile
  - Left/right is correct **≥90%** of the time on test walks
  - Beta users use it **≥3 days a week** while travelling

## Stage 2: Prove demand (Jun – Aug 2027)
**Risk to remove:** will strangers pay before the product exists?
- Run the **Kickstarter**: 30 days, with a goal of $250k (see `gtm/kickstarter/` and `finance/kickstarter-economics.md`).
- Build the pre-launch email list to 20,000+.
- **DVT** builds (production-intent design) and start certification testing.
- **Gate G2:**
  - **≥$1M** pledged
  - **≥3,000** backers
  - Pre-order cost per backer **< $60**

## Stage 3: Ship (Sep 2027 – Apr 2028)
**Risk to remove:** can we manufacture, certify and support the device at quality?
- Raise the **$6M** seed round.
- PVT (pilot production run), then mass production of the first **5,000 units**.
- Certification: FCC, CE/RED, UKCA and carrier approval.
- Expand from 1 to 8 cities. Launch the LORE Pass subscription and B2B pilots with hostels and tour operators.
- **Gate G3:**
  - Returns **< 5%**
  - Subscription **attach > 55%**
  - Monthly churn **< 6%**
  - App store rating **NPS > 50**

## What we will NOT do (focus)
- No glasses, no camera on the first models (the camera comes only with Atlas, after Square).
- No more than 1 new city per month until City Pack quality scores are measured.
- No retail distribution before the direct-to-customer return rate is under 5%.
