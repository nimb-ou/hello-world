# Operating Cadence

| Rhythm | What | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Daily (15 min) | Standup: what shipped yesterday, what ships today, blockers | Notes in Linear |
| Weekly (Mon) | Metrics review: waitlist, social, test walks, build status, cash | Dashboard snapshot in `company/` |
| Weekly (Fri) | Demo day: every team shows real work (a walk, a render, a schematic, a post) | Recording + decisions logged |
| Bi-weekly | Story quality review: listen to 10 random narrations; check fact accuracy and delight | Quality score per City Pack |
| Monthly | Investor/advisor update email; budget vs actuals | `finance/` update |
| Quarterly | OKR reset; board meeting (from seed onwards) | `company/okrs.md` |

**Rules**
1. Write it down, or it didn't happen.
2. Demos beat slides.
3. Every number has a source or is marked as an estimate.
4. The customer's walk is the unit of truth.
