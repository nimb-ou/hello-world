# Decisions Log

| # | Date | Decision | Why | What would change our mind |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | 2026-10 | Build a **dedicated device** (with a phone app as the prototype and companion), not an app alone | Phones are bad at 3 things travellers need: affordable global data (eSIM), all-day battery without draining the phone, and keeping the phone out of sight in unsafe places. A device also gives better gross margin and a brand moat. | If G0 shows the phone app alone gets ≥80% of the value, ship the app first and keep the device as a later premium model |
| D2 | 2026-10 | Name **LORE**, tagline "Every street has a story." | Short, means "stories and knowledge", sounds premium, works in many languages | The trademark clearance search finds a blocking conflict (see `legal-ops/ip-strategy.md`) |
| D3 | 2026-10 | **"Map Vision"** (sensor fusion + map geometry) instead of a camera on the first models | Avoids privacy problems, cost and battery drain from a camera. GNSS + IMU + building outlines are enough for left/right and "look up" | Field tests show left/right accuracy below 85% |
| D4 | 2026-10 | **Facts must be checked and sourced**; the AI tells the story but can't invent history | Trust is the product; invented history ruins it | — (core principle) |
| D5 | 2026-10 | **Rome** is City #1 | History per square metre, tourism volume, rich open data, reachable for walk tests | `research/launch-markets.md` scores another city much higher |
| D6 | 2026-10 | Three models: Pebble $149 / Square $299 / Atlas $549 + LORE Pass subscription | Good / better / best price ladder; the subscription brings recurring revenue | Research pricing study and Finance margin analysis |
| D7 | 2026-10 | Users bring their own earphones; LORE Buds are an optional accessory | Lower barrier to buying; avoids headphone design and battery complexity | — |
| D8 | 2026-10-31 | Atlas price raised **$549 → $599**; add an Explorer annual plan at $79 and a 30-day Trip Pass at $49 | Research pricing study: Atlas sits between Garmin inReach Mini 3 Plus ($499) and Ray-Ban Display / Watch Ultra ($799) | The survey shows $599 loses >20% of purchase intent compared with $549 |
| D9 | 2026-10-31 | London as a parallel development and beta city; Florence as "City 1.5" | Launch-market scoring: Rome 8.65, London 8.60, Paris 8.40 | — |
| D10 | 2026-10-31 | **Pebble becomes the "Lite" design**: it uses the phone for AI over Bluetooth, and offline it plays pre-generated stories, with no on-device LLM. Landed COGS about $90 → **39% margin at $149** | The full Pebble design cost $157 landed against a $149 price (−5%). The original concept already had Pebble tethered to the phone. Pre-generated stories don't need an on-device LLM | Beta users strongly want offline Q&A on the cheapest model |
| D11 | 2026-10-31 | **Square switches to LTE Cat-1bis** (instead of Cat-4) and stays at **$299** at launch. Cost-down target is landed cost ≤ $240 by unit 10k; $329 is the fallback price | Cat-1bis is enough for voice and text, is cheaper, and fixes the board-space conflict with the battery. Research shows $299 is the price-ladder anchor | The cost-down misses by more than 10% at DVT → price $329 |
| D12 | 2026-10-31 | **Atlas: 13.5 mm thick; marketing claims "2+ days" battery** (not 3) | Simulation: 2.4 days with optimised firmware. We don't overclaim | A bigger battery cell fits at DVT |
| D13 | 2026-10-31 | Assemble in **Vietnam/Malaysia**, not mainland China | Tariff exposure: Square costs $343 landed if assembled in China | Tariff policy changes |
