# Kickstarter Update Cadence & Backer Community Plan

## 1. Principles
1. **Never go silent.** Delivery anxiety comes from silence. We post at least one update a month until every reward ships, even when nothing has changed.
2. **Bad news early, with a reason and a new date.** Hardware tells the CMO first, and backers hear within 7 days.
3. **Show the work.** Every update has at least one real photo or video from the lab, the factory or Rome. Renders are labelled.
4. **Backers are co-creators.** Votes, beta access and story suggestions make them feel part of building it.

## 2. Update cadence

| # | Day | Title (draft) | Content |
|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | D3 | "Your questions, answered" | Top 10 comment questions; links to FAQ changes |
| 3 | D7 | "Inside the DVT build" | Photos of DVT units, the next stretch goal reveal |
| 4 | D10 | "New: the Rome bundle" | Add-on reveal; colour poll results |
| 5 | D14 | "Halfway: vote for the next city" | Florence vs Venice poll (stretch at $700k) |
| 6 | D17 | "Meet the story team" | How a fact card is made; a sample source trail |
| 7 | D21 | "Field report: left/right accuracy" | The beta accuracy report, with methodology, honest numbers and known limits |
| 8 | D24 | "LORE in your city" | Map of backers by city; the next City Pack priority signal |
| 9 | D28 | "48 hours: last chance for Early Bird prices" | Remaining limited tiers; recap |
| 10 | D30 | "Thank you, [N] founding walkers" | Final numbers, what happens next, pledge-manager timeline |

## 3. Comment & message SLAs
- Kickstarter comments: reply in **< 4 hours** from 06:00–24:00 CET during the campaign, and < 24 h afterwards.
- Direct messages: < 24 h.
- Tone: warm, specific, never defensive. If we don't know, we say "we don't know yet, and here's when we will."
- Escalation: hardware or schedule questions go to the Hardware lead, refund requests to Ops, and press to the CMO.

## 4. Backer community: "Founding Walkers"

| Space | Purpose | Moderation |
|---|---|---|
| **Discord: "LORE Founding Walkers"** | #announcements, #ask-the-team, #city-requests, #story-suggestions, #beta-feedback, #show-your-walks, plus regional channels (#rome, #london, #nyc…) | Community manager + 6 volunteer moderators from backers (LORE Locals pipeline) |
| **Kickstarter comments** | The official channel for every backer | Team |
| **Backer-only beta** (TestFlight / Play beta) | Phone-app prototype with simulation mode, Rome & Lisbon packs; 1,000 seats, first come, first served from VIPs | Product |
| **Monthly "Walk with us" livestream** | A 30-minute live walk in Rome with Q&A | Content lead |
| **City vote** | Backers rank the next 10 cities; results published | CMO |
| **Story suggestions** | Backers submit "a story my city deserves". The Story team verifies before any use, and the contributor is credited with consent | Story team |

**Rituals**
- **Founding Walker number.** Every backer gets a number (#0001…) shown in the app at activation.
- **"First walk" photo wall.** Once units ship, backers post their first walk in #show-your-walks, and we feature one a week.
- **Backer credits** inside each City Pack ("Founding Walkers").

**Community health KPIs:** Discord joins as a % of backers (target 20%), weekly active members (target 25% of members), update open rate (target 55%+), and the share of comments answered within SLA (target 95%).

## 5. Escalation playbook (if the campaign underperforms)
| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| D1 < $180k | Pause cold prospecting ads, keep retargeting; bring 10 micro creators forward; publish "Funded in X days" honestly |
| D7 < $450k | Introduce the limited "Rome Founders" Terracotta Square colourway (if Hardware approves the CMF cost) or a bundle; second press wave |
| D14 < $560k | Founder livestream + AMA week; partner newsletter swaps with other live Kickstarter projects (cross-promotion is common practice; only with projects we would genuinely recommend) |
| Any day ROAS < 2.0 for 48h | Cut that ad set; move budget to creators or retargeting |
| Negative press or a viral criticism | Respond within 24h with facts, never with deletion. Founder posts the reply |
