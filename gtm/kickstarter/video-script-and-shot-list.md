# Kickstarter Campaign Video — Script & Shot List (2:30)

**Working title:** "Look Up"
**Runtime:** 2:30 (150 s); a 0:60 cut-down and three 0:15 hooks are taken from the same shoot
**Shoot:** Rome, 3 days in early April 2027 (after Easter crowds; golden hour around 19:30 CEST). Locations are public streets, which need permits for crew filming. Video lead to apply to Roma Capitale's film office (Roma Lazio Film Commission).¹ No drones over the historic centre without ENAC authorisation (no-fly restrictions).
**Talent:** Founder (Nimit Jain), 1 lead traveller (cast; paid, signed release), 4–6 real beta testers (signed releases, real reactions only)
**Voice of LORE:** the final product TTS voice. Never a fake human voice-over presented as the device.
**Look:** Leica-style natural light, 35 mm and 50 mm primes, warm grade (Bone highlights, Ink shadows, Terracotta accents). Typography in Fraunces for titles and Inter for supers.
**Music:** licensed, an acoustic, slightly cinematic track (Musicbed / Artlist). Starts sparse and lifts at 1:05.
**Truth rules:** any device shown functioning must be a real working unit or labelled "Prototype shown" or "Render". Every story heard in the video must come from the verified Rome City Pack. The "plague hospital" line is a placeholder until the Story team confirms a real, sourced building.
