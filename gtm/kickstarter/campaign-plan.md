# LORE on Kickstarter — Campaign Plan

**Campaign window:** Tuesday 25 May 2027, 15:00 CEST / 09:00 ET → Thursday 24 June 2027, 15:00 CEST (30 days)
**Funding goal (public):** **$250,000** (the operating-plan figure; covers tooling deposits plus the first MOQ for Pebble)
**Internal targets (Gate G2):** ≥ **$1,000,000** pledged · ≥ **3,000** backers · pre-order cost per backer **< $60**
**Base-case forecast:** $1.04M / 4,600 backers / CPB ≈ $50 (see `../gtm-strategy.md` §7)

All prices below are **planning prices (est.)**, derived from COMPANY.md MSRPs. Finance must sign them off against the BOM and landed cost (`finance/kickstarter-economics.md`) before the page goes live.

## 1. Why this goal

- **$250k public goal:** low enough to fund in the first hours. Our base case needs about $300k on day 1, and "funded in X hours" is itself a PR headline and a social-proof driver. It is high enough to be honest: it is the smallest raise that pays for injection-mould tooling for the Pebble enclosure plus a first production run. Hardware team to confirm the figure.
- **We will not launch** unless the waitlist is at least 15,000 *and* VIP reservations are at least 1,200 on 18 May 2027 (go/no-go meeting, T–7). If not, we move the launch by 2–4 weeks rather than risk a slow start.

**Tier design logic**
- **Anchor on Square.** Square is the "why buy" SKU: global eSIM, screen and 2 days of battery. The Super Early Bird at $199 is the headline price in ads ("from $99" for Pebble in TikTok hooks).
- **Limited early-bird counts** are sized so that about 60% of day-1 backers land in a limited tier, which creates urgency in VIP emails. VIP $1 reservers get a **48-hour exclusive window** on T2/T5/T8, so the Super Early Bird tiers are only visible to the public after VIPs have had first access. In practice we do this by launching with T2/T5/T8 open and telling VIPs the launch time 2 hours before the public email. Kickstarter has no true private tiers, so we only state what we can enforce.
- **Bundles lift average pledge** by $40–60 (est.). The Traveller Kit is the "most popular" badge candidate.
- **Avoid tier sprawl:** 16 tiers is at the upper end. If Kickstarter staff or user testing show confusion, we merge T3/T4 and T6/T7.
