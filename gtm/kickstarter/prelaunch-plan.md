# Kickstarter Pre-Launch List-Building Plan

**Target:** 20,000 double-opt-in emails and 2,000 $1 VIP reservations by Mon 24 May 2027, plus 3,000+ followers of the Kickstarter pre-launch page.
**Expected list conversion:** 5–10% of the full list becomes backers. Base case: 8%, or about 1,600 backers including VIPs (see the conversion math in §6).
**Budget:** $55,000 paid media: about $33k for cold lead generation (12,000 paid leads at a blended ~$2.75 CPL) and about $22k for retargeting the list and visitors toward the $1 VIP reservation and Kickstarter "Notify me". Creator seeding comes from the creator budget.

Benchmarks: crowdfunding Meta lead costs of $1.27–$4.83 per lead and $4.74–$31.55 per $1 reservation, from LaunchBoom case studies.¹ An independent board-game agency targets a $1.50–$3.00 CPL.¹ Practitioners warn that CPL can jump several-fold without creative rotation.¹

## 3. The pre-launch funnel

```
Ad / content  →  lore.travel/early (lead page)  →  Thank-you page = $1 VIP offer  →  Welcome sequence  →  KS "Notify me"
                                   │                               │
                       email only (~84%)                      VIP $1 (~10–12% of leads)
```

**Lead page (lore.travel/early)** — the page `../website/index.html` in "early" mode
- Headline: "Every street has a story. Hear it." Sub: "LORE is launching on Kickstarter in May. Join the list for up to 34% off."
- One field (email), with the country inferred from IP. Double opt-in for GDPR (ePrivacy rules plus Italian Garante practice). The consent checkbox is unticked by default.
- Social proof: waitlist count, press logos *only after coverage exists*, and beta-tester quotes *only with signed releases*.

**$1 VIP reservation (thank-you page)**
- Offer: "Reserve your Super Early Bird for $1. VIPs get the launch link 2 hours before everyone else, plus a free Founding Backer clip (KS add-on credit, $15 value). Refundable any time."
- Checkout through Stripe on lore.travel. The $1 is fully refundable on request, and is refunded *automatically* if we don't launch by 31 Aug 2027.
- Why: deposit holders are reported to convert far better than email-only leads. LaunchBoom claims up to 30× (a vendor claim). An agency reports 35–40% day-1 backing for $1 reservers vs 20–25% for email leads (board games, a higher-converting category).¹ We plan conservatively at 30% for VIPs and 4% for non-VIPs.

## 4. Referral "skip the queue" programme (pre-launch)

Tool: Viral Loops, KickoffLabs or a custom build (est. $50–100/mo).

| Referrals | Reward |
|---|---|
| 1 | Move up 100 places + "Secrets of Rome" PDF zine |
| 3 | Founding Backer clip (free add-on) |
| 10 | Free leather lanyard (add-on credit) |
| 25 | Free LORE Buds (add-on credit) |
| Top 5 referrers (by 24 May) | A LORE Atlas Founders Edition each |

Fraud controls: a referral counts only after double opt-in, and we block disposable email domains and duplicate IPs in bursts. Rewards are delivered only to backers, so the cost is concentrated in converted fans.

## 5. Creative plan for paid lead gen

Six new creatives a week. Three hooks are tested in every format.

| Angle | Hook (first 2 seconds) | Format | Persona |
|---|---|---|---|
| Secret | "This window in Rome was bricked up for a reason." | 9:16 UGC walk, device on bag strap | P1 |
| Eyes up | "I walked Rome for 6 hours and never took my phone out." | Talking-head creator + B-roll | P1/P4 |
| Roaming pain | "My last trip's roaming bill: €112. This has data in 150+ countries." | Text-on-screen + product macro | P1/P3 |
| Safety | "Long-press. That's it. Someone you trust knows where you are." | Night-walk POV (no fear-mongering) | P1/P4 |
| Retiree | "My dad hates apps. He loved this." | 4:5 family feed video | P2 |
| Design | "Leica meets a Roman cobblestone." | Macro product + Bone/Terracotta stills | Design lovers |
| Price | "From $99 on Kickstarter, 25 May." (W10+) | Static + countdown | All |

Audiences: interests (solo travel, Lonely Planet, Rick Steves, Airalo, Leica, Teenage Engineering, Monocle), engaged-shopper and frequent-international-traveller behaviours, and a 1–3% lookalike of the waitlist. Geos: US 50%, UK 15%, DE/NL/Nordics 15%, CA/AU 12%, rest of EU 8%. We start with Italy excluded from paid: Italians aren't the core buyer for a Rome pack, and they're served by the launch event. Revisit later.

Kill and scale rules: after $150 spend, pause any creative with CPL > 1.5× median. Scale +20%/day when CPL < 0.8× median and LP conversion is ≥15%. Refresh any creative with frequency > 2.5 in 7 days.

## 6. Conversion math (list → backers)

| Segment | Size | Conversion over 30 days | Backers |
|---|---|---|---|
| VIP $1 reservers | 2,000 | 30% (est.) | 600 |
| Kickstarter followers who are not VIP (overlap removed) | ~2,000 | 15% (est.) | 300 |
| Remaining email list | 16,000 | 4.4% (est.) | 700 |
| **Total from list** | **20,000** | **8.0%** | **1,600** |

That is within the 5–10% brief. Sensitivity: at 5% the list yields 1,000 backers and we need about 3,600 more from ads, creators and Kickstarter organic. That is the conservative case, which still hits ≥3,000 backers but not $1M.

## 7. List health rules
- Double opt-in. Sunset non-openers after 60 days into a "last chance" re-permission flow, then suppress them before launch so they don't hurt deliverability.
- Warm up the sending domain (send.lore.travel) from Feb 2027 with SPF, DKIM, DMARC, BIMI-ready.
- Launch-day sends are split into waves of 5k by engagement tier: VIP, then high-engaged, then the rest.

## 8. Weekly scorecard (pre-launch)
Leads (paid/organic), CPL by channel, LP conversion, VIP rate, cost per VIP, welcome-email open/click, KS followers, referral K-factor (referred leads ÷ referring leads), and unsubscribes. These are defined in `../metrics.md`.
