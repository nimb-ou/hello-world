## 1. The GTM thesis in five sentences

1. **Rome first, story first.** We win attention by showing a secret about a street people already know ("Secrets of Rome"). We don't lead with a spec sheet. The content works before the device exists, so the audience grows during Stage 0.
2. **A device people can explain at a dinner party.** "It's a pebble that clips to your bag. It tells you the story of the building on your left through your own earbuds, and it works without roaming." Every message has to fit that sentence.
3. **Crowdfunding is the demand test and the first factory order.** We launch on Kickstarter on **Tue 25 May 2027** for 30 days with a **$250k goal**. Gate G2 is **≥$1M pledged, ≥3,000 backers, and pre-order cost per backer below $60**.
4. **Direct to consumer before retail.** We go DTC at lore.travel (Q4 2027). We pitch airport retail and design stores only after DTC returns are below 5% (operating-plan rule). B2B fleet rental for hostels, tour operators and cruise excursions runs in parallel as pilots from Q4 2027.
5. **Trust is the moat, so it is also the marketing.** Every story has a source, so every content piece can show its receipt ("Source: Archivio di Stato di Roma"). That beats tour-bus megaphones and AI tools that make things up.

## 2. Positioning

### 2.1 Positioning statement
**For** curious solo travellers who want to *really* see a city, **LORE is** a pocket-sized AI travel companion **that** tells you the true story of what's around you (left, right, ahead, look up) through your own earphones, online or off. **Unlike** audio-guide apps that drain your phone and need signal, or generic AI chatbots that invent history, **LORE** fuses map geometry with a sourced story engine, has built-in global data and a 2–3 day battery, and keeps your phone safely in your pocket.

### 2.2 Category we claim
We call it **"AI travel companion"** in titles and search. We avoid "AI pin" or "AI wearable": that category is tainted by the Humane AI Pin shutdown and the Rabbit R1 reviews.¹ Secondary descriptor: "a smart audio guide for the whole city."

### 2.3 Competitive frame (the "instead of" set)

| What travellers use today | Their pain | LORE's line |
|---|---|---|
| Phone audio-guide apps (VoiceMap, GPSmyCity, Rick Steves Audio Europe, SmartGuide) | Battery drain, roaming, phone out in crowds, fixed routes | "No route. No roaming. No phone in hand." |
| Google Maps + Wikipedia while walking | Head down, screen-reading, no context about *which* building | "Eyes up. LORE knows which side the story is on." |
| Museum or site audio-guide rental (e.g. Louvre audio guide is paid per visit²) | One site only, hygiene, a queue for the device | "One companion for the whole city, every day of the trip." |
| Group walking tour | Fixed time, group pace, €20–60 per tour | "Your own private guide, at your pace, at 2 a.m. if you like." |
| Generic AI assistant on the phone | Hallucinated history, no sense of where you're facing | "Every story is checked and sourced." |
| Travel eSIM (Airalo etc.) + battery bank | Solves data and power, but tells no stories | "Data and stories in one thing that lasts 2–3 days." |

### 2.4 Proof points we must be able to show at launch
- **Left/right accuracy** of at least 90% on test walks (Gate G1). We publish the methodology.
- **Rome City Pack v1:** 300+ places and 1,000+ fact cards, 100% sourced, at least 98% passing the fact check (OKR Q4 2026).
- **Battery:** measured hours on a published usage profile. We never say "2 days" without the profile.
- **Sean Ellis score:** at least 40% "very disappointed". We publish it, with the sample size.

**Message hierarchy by depth**
- **3 words:** Hear the city.
- **8 words:** The stories of every street, in your earphones.
- **25 words:** LORE is a pocket-sized AI companion that tells you the true story of what's around you through your own earphones, with global data, offline maps and a 2-day battery.
- **Proof line:** "The building on your right was a plague hospital in 1665. Look up at the bricked-up third-floor windows." *(This is the canonical product demo line from COMPANY.md. Content must use only facts checked in the City Pack. Swap in a verified Rome fact before publishing.)*

**Words we use:** story, street, look up, local friend, walk, true, sourced, quietly, pocket, eyes up.
**Words we avoid:** revolutionary, game-changer, AI-powered everything, "never get lost again" (not true offline in every case), "100% safe" (no safety claim we cannot evidence), "replaces your guide" (guides are future partners).

**Safety-claim rule (with Legal):** we say "SOS button, live location sharing and timed check-ins". We never say "keeps you safe". Satellite SOS is Atlas-only and depends on coverage, so it always carries an asterisk.

**Blended DTC CAC ceiling 2028: $70 (est.).** Rationale: LORE Pass attach target is >55% at $9.99/mo after the free 3 months (G3). Contribution from the subscription alone over a 12-month life is about 0.55 × 9 months × $9.99 × ~70% margin (est.) ≈ $35, plus hardware gross margin (Finance to confirm). CAC ≤ $70 keeps payback within the first purchase plus 6 months.

## 7. Funnel model (Kickstarter base case)

Spreadsheet-ready numbers. All are estimates and are re-forecast weekly in `metrics.md`.

```
PRE-LAUNCH (1 Mar – 24 May 2027)
 Paid impressions          ~3.7M   ($33k cold lead-gen at ~$9 blended CPM)
 → Landing-page visits     ~60k    (link CTR ~1.6%)
 → Email leads (paid)      ~12k    (LP conversion ~20% on cold paid traffic → CPL ≈ $2.75)
 + Organic/social/PR leads ~8k     (Stage-0 waitlist 5k, creators, referral, Reddit, press)
 (+ $22k retargeting the list/visitors → $1 VIP and KS "Notify me")
 = Email list              20,000
 → $1 VIP reservations     2,000   (10% of list; LaunchBoom-style deposit page)
 → KS page followers       3,000+  (asked in welcome email #2)

LAUNCH (25 May – 24 Jun 2027)
 VIP → backers             600     (30%)
 Non-VIP list → backers    720     (4% of 18k)
 Campaign ads → backers    1,400   ($70k at $50 CPA; ROAS ~4.5 at $225 avg pledge)
 Creators + PR → backers   850
 Kickstarter organic       1,030   ("Projects We Love", search, category browse; ~22% of backers, est.)
 = Total backers           4,600
 × Avg pledge              $225    (mix: 45% Pebble, 40% Square, 8% Atlas, 7% bundles)
 = Pledged                 ~$1.04M  (G2 ≥ $1M  ✔ ; backers ≥ 3,000 ✔)
 Cost per backer           $230k / 4,600 = $50  (G2 < $60 ✔)
```

**Scenarios**

| | Conservative | Base | Upside |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email list | 14,000 | 20,000 | 28,000 |
| Backers | 2,700 | 4,600 | 7,200 |
| Avg pledge | $205 | $225 | $250 |
| Pledged | $554k | $1.04M | $1.8M |
| Marketing spend | $190k (ads paused at low ROAS) | $230k | $290k (scaled at ROAS ≥3) |
| CPB | $70 ✖ | $50 ✔ | $40 ✔ |

**Leading indicator: day 1.** Crowdfunding practitioners often say that the first day is roughly a third of the final total ("first day × 3").¹² Our base case needs **≥$300k in the first 24 hours**. If day 1 is below $180k, we switch to the conservative playbook: we cut ads to retargeting only, push creators, and add a mid-campaign "Rome Founders" limited tier.

### Funnel stage definitions (shared with `metrics.md`)
1. **Aware:** reached (impressions, video views ≥3s)
2. **Engaged:** ≥50% video watch, profile visit, save or share
3. **Lead:** email sign-up (double opt-in)
4. **Hot lead:** $1 VIP reservation, *or* Kickstarter follower, *or* 2+ email clicks
5. **Backer / customer**
6. **Activated:** first walk completed with the device (post-ship)
7. **Subscriber:** LORE Pass paid after the free 3 months
8. **Advocate:** referral sent / review posted / LORE Local

## 9. Organisation and cadence (GTM)
- **Weekly growth stand-up (Mon):** CPL, CPB, list growth, top 3 creatives, experiments.
- **Content team (Stage 0–2):** content lead (hire, per operating plan) plus 1 contract video editor in Rome and 1 community manager (part-time).
- **Creative testing:** 6 new ad creatives a week in pre-launch. We kill a creative whose CPL is >1.5× the median after $150 of spend.
- **Experiment log:** every test has a hypothesis, metric and decision, kept in a Notion or Sheets log.

## 10. Risks to the GTM plan

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meta CPL inflates over the 12-week pre-launch (practitioner reports show sharp rises after creative fatigue⁶) | High | High | 6 new creatives/week; TikTok Spark Ads; creator whitelisting; organic Reddit |
| "AI gadget" scepticism after Humane/Rabbit | Medium | High | Lead with stories and safety, not AI; show real walks; publish accuracy data |
| Beta slips (G1) and compresses pre-launch | Medium | High | Content engine is phone-prototype-based; KS date moves as a block (+4 weeks), never launch without working-unit footage |
| A fact error goes viral | Low | High | Only City-Pack-verified facts in content; corrections policy; source on screen |
| Delivery-risk scepticism (crowdfunding hardware delays) | High | Medium | Honest "Risks & challenges" section, a named contract manufacturer status (no fake partners), monthly build updates |
| Safety-claim liability | Low | High | Legal-approved safety copy only (Section 3) |
