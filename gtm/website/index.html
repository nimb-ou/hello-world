How it works

Phone in your pocket. Eyes on the city.

01 · CLIP IT ON

Wear it, forget it

Clip LORE to a strap, lanyard or wrist strap. Pair your own Bluetooth earphones (LE Audio or Classic), or use the small built-in speaker.

02 · JUST WALK

Stories find you

LORE knows which way you're facing and tells you 20–40 second stories about what's around you. Say “tell me more” to go deeper. It goes quiet when you cross a road.

03 · ONE BUTTON

Ask anything

Press to ask. Double-press for “what's around me”. Long-press for SOS.

PRESS×2HOLD · SOS
Map Vision

No camera. It still knows what's on your left.

LORE combines precise positioning, motion sensing and the real shape of the streets, so it can say left, right, ahead, behind and look up.

  • Dual-band GNSSL1 + L5 satellite positioning, which holds up better in narrow old streets
  • 9-axis IMU + compassWhich way you're facing and turning
  • Step detectionWalking, stopping, turning back
  • Map geometryBuilding footprints, street sides, entrances
  • Places & live dataOpening hours, closures, what's nearby

FAQ

Good questions.

When can I get one?

We plan to launch on Kickstarter in 2027, with the first units shipping from late 2027 (estimated). Waitlist members hear first.

Do I need my phone?

Only for setup, through the LORE app. Square and Atlas then work on their own with built-in data. Pebble uses your phone's connection over Bluetooth, but your phone can stay in your bag.

Which earphones work?

Any Bluetooth earphones (Classic A2DP or LE Audio). In cities we recommend open-ear headphones so you can hear traffic.

Does it work without signal?

Yes, with a downloaded City Pack: offline maps, routing, stories and on-device AI. Live information needs data.

Which cities?

Rome first. Next on the roadmap: Lisbon, Paris, London, Barcelona, Istanbul, Kyoto and New York.

Is my location private?

Location is processed on the device wherever possible, and we never sell location data. Sharing is off until you turn it on.
