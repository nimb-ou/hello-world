# PR Plan

**Budget:** $25k shared with the Rome launch event (PR freelancer ~$9k for Mar–Jun 2027, press kits/samples $3k, event $13k).
**Goal:** 15+ tier-1/2 articles during the campaign, 3+ "Funded in X hours"-type follow-ups, and backlinks to lore.travel for SEO.
**Note:** the publications listed are **targets**. We have no existing relationships, and individual journalist contacts will be researched (via Muck Rack/Prowly) and not fabricated here.

## 1. Narrative angles
1. **"The anti-AI-pin":** after the Humane AI Pin was shut down,¹ here's a narrow, useful AI device that does one job: tell you about where you are, with sourced facts. (Tech press)
2. **"An AI that isn't allowed to make up history":** the fact-card standard. (Tech + culture press)
3. **"Eyes up in overtouristed Rome":** 22.9M arrivals in 2025, a record;² a calmer way to experience a crowded city. (Travel press)
4. **"Quiet-luxury hardware":** Leica × Teenage Engineering × Aesop design language, titanium Atlas. (Design press)
5. **"Solo travel is booming":** solo-safety features without fear marketing. (Lifestyle/women's press)
6. **"Founder builds in Rome":** the startup story, Kickstarter as a demand test. (Startup press, Italian press)

## 4. Rules
- No coverage is ever claimed before it exists ("as seen in" logos only after publication).
- Review units only with honest prototype labelling; there is a return/swap policy.
- Corrections: if a journalist reports a spec wrong, we correct it politely within 24h.
- Spokespeople: the Founder (primary), the CMO (GTM questions), the Story lead (fact methodology).

## 5. Measurement
Articles by tier, estimated reach (unique visitors per month of the outlet, from Similarweb), referral sessions and pledges per article (Kickstarter referral tags / UTMs), share of voice vs "AI travel guide" terms, backlinks (Ahrefs DR).
