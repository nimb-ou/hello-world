# Rome Launch Event Concept: "Look Up, Roma"

**When:** Tuesday 25 May 2027 (Kickstarter D1), 17:00–22:00 CEST, plus press preview walks 10:00–13:00
**Where:** historic centre. A walk starts at Piazza Navona and ends at a rented courtyard or rooftop venue nearby (to be scouted; options include palazzo courtyards that hire for events; no venue is booked or implied).
**Budget:** ≈ $13,000 (est.) from the PR/event line
**Audience:** 30 press/creators (in person), 120 waitlist VIPs from Rome (RSVP by lottery), 20 beta testers, and a livestream to the online waitlist.

## Concept
A launch you *walk* rather than watch. Guests get a LORE (DVT unit, numbered) and their own earbuds (or a loaner open-ear pair), and walk a 45-minute "Look Up" route in small groups of 10 with a team member. The finale is a courtyard gathering at golden hour (sunset in Rome on 25 May is about 20:35), where the "Funded in X hours" moment is celebrated live.

## Rules and logistics
- No amplified sound or branding installations in public squares without permits; walks are small groups using earbuds (silent by design).
- Accessibility: one step-free route variant.
- Data capture: RSVP on lore.travel with consent; photo consent at check-in.
- Backup: if rain, a short route under porticoes plus extended courtyard demos.

## KPIs
Pledges from the event referral tag, content pieces produced (target 40+ creator posts), press attendance (target 15), livestream views.
