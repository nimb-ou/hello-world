# LORE Creator Programme

**Budget (Nov 2026 – Jun 2027):** $40,000 cash + about 60 seeded units (EVT/DVT; loaned, so they count as COGS, not cash).
**Goal:** about 850 Kickstarter backers attributable to creators + PR (with ~600 from creators alone at ≤$45 CPB) and 1,500 pre-launch leads.
**Rule:** we have **no creator partnerships yet**. Every name below is a *prospect category or example of the landscape*. We may not imply endorsement until a contract is signed. Every paid post carries a disclosure (#ad / "Paid partnership" label), per FTC Endorsement Guides and the UK ASA/CAP influencer guidance.¹

## 1. Tiers

| Tier | Followers | # creators | Compensation (est., per deliverable set) | Deliverables | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nano "Locals"** | 2k–15k | 20 | Product (kept after launch) + 15% affiliate commission on KS/DTC sales via code; $0–250 | 1 TikTok/Reel + 3 Stories | Rome/Lisbon-based storytellers, authenticity, UGC for ads |
| **Micro** | 15k–100k | 10 | $500–2,500 + 10% affiliate | 1 Reel/TikTok + 1 carousel or Short + Stories; 90-day paid usage rights (whitelisting) | Core performance driver; Spark/Partnership ads |
| **Mid** | 100k–500k | 4 | $3,000–8,000 | 1 dedicated video + link in bio during the campaign | Reach + credibility |
| **Macro / YouTube** | 500k+ | 1–2 | $10,000–25,000 for a 60–90s YouTube integration (negotiate; some only do flat fees) | Integrated segment in a travel/tech video during campaign week 1–2 | Spike + long-tail search |

Benchmarks: published 2025 rate guides vary widely. Instagram micro $2k–8k vs $35–450 depending on the source; TikTok micro $200–1,200; YouTube macro $25k–49k (agency) vs $1.1k–20k (marketplace).² Travel influencers typically see engagement rates of 4–9% (IG) and 10–16% (TikTok), per an InfluenceFlow guide (treat as directional).³ We pay against expected **views and engagement, not followers**. Max CPM we will pay is $25 for micro and $35 for YouTube integrations (est.).

**Budget split:** Nano $4k · Micro $14k · Mid $12k · Macro $10k = $40k.

## 2. Creator landscape (prospect categories, to be researched and vetted)
- **Rome/Italy storytellers & guides on TikTok/IG:** licensed Rome guides who post history (best fit, and a future B2B channel).
- **Solo female travel creators** (P1): safety-conscious, practical.
- **Travel YouTube** (P2/P3): e.g. channels in the landscape such as Drew Binsky, Kara and Nate, and Mark Wiens (food) are reference points for scale, not targets we have contacted.⁴
- **One-bag / travel-gear reviewers** (r/onebag audience, YouTube gear channels): the most direct product fit.
- **Design/tech reviewers** (minimalist gadget channels): design credibility.
- **Accessibility creators** (later, for LORE Access): paid co-design, not just promotion.

Vetting checklist: audience geo ≥60% in US/UK/EU/CA/AU, real engagement (HypeAuditor/Modash audit; fake-follower share <15%), brand safety check of the last 50 posts, no conflicting deals with direct competitors, and comfort with our truth rules (no fabricated history).

## 3. Brief template (one page)
- **Campaign:** "Look Up" pre-launch (Mar–May 2027) / "Live on Kickstarter" (25 May–24 Jun 2027)
- **One message:** LORE lets you see the city with your eyes up: stories from what's around you, through your own earphones.
- **Must show:** the device on a strap/clip, a real walk, the creator *looking up*, and the phone staying in a pocket.
- **Must say:** "This is a prototype" (until DVT), "#ad", and the Kickstarter date or link + code.
- **Must not:** claim it "keeps you safe"; invent facts (use only the story cards we provide, or check with us); show it in use while crossing roads; show battery or spec numbers we haven't published.
- **Creative freedom:** hook, edit and humour are yours. We approve facts only (24h turnaround).
- **Deliverables & dates:** listed per contract. **Usage rights:** 90 days of paid usage (micro+).
- **Tracking:** unique link `lore.travel/c/<handle>` → UTM → Kickstarter referral tag; code `<HANDLE>` in BackerKit add-ons.

## 4. Tracking & attribution
| Layer | Tool | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-launch leads | UTM (`utm_source=creator&utm_campaign=<handle>`) on lore.travel/early | Count leads + VIPs per creator |
| Kickstarter | Kickstarter custom referral tags (Creator Dashboard → Referrals) | Pledges per tag |
| DTC | Shopify affiliate (e.g. UpPromote/Refersion/Impact) | 30-day cookie |
| Brand lift | Comment sentiment, saves, follower delta in the 72h after a post | Weekly creator scorecard |

Creator scorecard (weekly): views, ER, link clicks, leads, VIPs, backers, $ pledged, CPB, CPM. **Re-book rule:** CPB ≤ $45 or CPM ≤ $15. **Drop rule:** CPB > $90 after two posts.

## 5. Seeding programme (EVT → DVT units)
- Feb–Apr 2027: 10 EVT units go to Rome-based nano/micro creators and guides under NDA, with no posting obligation before the embargo (12 Apr).
- May 2027: 25 DVT units go to micro/mid creators for launch-week content.
- Units are loaned, then swapped for a production unit at shipping.

## 6. Contracts
Standard agreement: deliverables, dates, disclosure, fact-approval right, usage rights, payment 50/50 (on signing / on posting), kill fee 25%, and morality and accuracy clauses. Legal templates to come from `legal-ops/`.
