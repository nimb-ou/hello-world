# LORE launches on Kickstarter: a pocket-sized AI travel companion that tells you the true story of every street

**Rome-built device uses "Map Vision" to know what's on your left, right and above your head, with fact-checked, sourced stories, built-in global data and offline City Packs. Early backers can reserve it from $99.**

ROME and WILMINGTON, Del., 25 May 2027: LORE Labs today launched **LORE** on Kickstarter, a pocket-sized travel companion that tells travellers the stories of the city around them through their own earphones. LORE lets people explore a new city with their phone in their pocket and their eyes on the streets.

LORE clips to a bag strap, lanyard or wrist strap. As the wearer walks, it works out which way they are facing and what is around them, and it tells 20–40 second stories about the buildings, squares and details they would otherwise walk past. One physical button controls everything: press to ask a question, double-press for "what's around me", and long-press for SOS.

**Map Vision, not a camera.** The Pebble and Square models have no camera. LORE "sees" by fusing dual-band GNSS (L1 + L5), a 9-axis IMU and compass, step and walking-direction detection, and map geometry such as building footprints and entrances. **Stories that are checked, not invented.** LORE's story engine may only use fact cards that the LORE story team has checked, each with a cited source. The launch Rome City Pack contains **[300+]** places and **[1,000+]** fact cards.

**Built for travel.** The Square and Atlas models include built-in global eSIM data for **150+ countries** [final list to be published before shipping]. All models support offline City Packs and have a target battery life of 1.5 to 3 days, which leaves the phone's battery for tickets, photos and payments. Safety features include SOS, live location sharing and timed "walk me home" check-ins. The titanium Atlas adds satellite SOS (coverage-dependent).
