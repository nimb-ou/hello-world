**Trigger:** double opt-in confirmed on lore.travel. **ESP:** Klaviyo (or Mailchimp). **Sender:** "Nimit at LORE" <hello@lore.travel>, plain-ish design (Bone background, Fraunces headline, one image, one CTA).
**Benchmarks:** Klaviyo reports automated-flow averages of ~48.6% opens and ~4.7% clicks (2025); welcome emails are among the highest-opening flows (~54%, 2024).¹ **Our targets:** E1 ≥ 60% open / ≥ 10% click; flow average ≥ 50% / ≥ 6%.

### E2: Why we're building a device (and not just an app)
Phones are brilliant, but they're bad at three things when you travel:
1. **Data abroad:** roaming bills or SIM juggling. LORE Square and Atlas have **global eSIM data in 150+ countries** built in.
2. **Battery:** your phone holds your boarding pass, tickets, payments and photos. LORE has a **1.5–3 day battery** (target, by model), so your phone keeps its charge.
3. **Being in your hand:** heads-down in a crowd is the least safe and least present way to see a city.

So we're building a small thing you clip on: one button, your own earphones, and stories about what's on your left, right and above your head. [See how it works →]

### E4: Our AI isn't allowed to make up history
Most AI will confidently invent a "fact" about a building. Ours can't. Every LORE story is built from **fact cards** that our team checks, each with a source you can see. The AI's job is to tell it well, in 20–40 seconds, in a voice like a curious friend.

### E5: For anyone who walks home alone
Long-press LORE for **SOS**: your chosen contacts get your live location. Set **walk me home**: if you don't check in by your chosen time, someone you trust is told. **Live location sharing** is off until you turn it on, and only shared with the people you choose.
What we won't do: promise LORE "keeps you safe". It's a tool, not an emergency service. In the EU, 112 is the number to know.
