# Community: LORE Locals, Referrals & the Storyteller Marketplace

## 1. LORE Locals (ambassador programme)

**What:** a network of city-based enthusiasts (licensed guides, history students, long-term residents, superfan backers) who represent LORE in their city, host walks, find stories and give product feedback.
**Launch:** Rome pilot with 15 Locals in Feb 2027 (beta phase). Scaling to each new City Pack city (target: 10–20 Locals per city by 2028).

| Level | How you get there | Perks | Asks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Local** | Apply + a short call; 1 walk hosted or 3 stories suggested | Free LORE Pass Explorer while active, a Locals badge in the app, early beta access, the Locals Discord | 1 activity/month (walk, story suggestion, content post, feedback session) |
| **Guide Local** | 3 months active + 5 verified stories accepted | A free device (Square), a 15% affiliate commission on their referral code, co-branded "walk with [Name]" in the app | Host 1 public walk/month, onboard new Locals |
| **City Lead** (1–2 per city) | Invitation | A paid stipend (est. €300/month), City Pack credits, an input to the city roadmap | Run the city community, moderate, coordinate the launch event |

Rules: Locals disclose their relationship when posting (#LOREPartner / "LORE Local"). Story suggestions go through the Story team's verification; Locals are credited (with consent) on the fact card. No fake reviews, ever: Locals must not post product reviews on stores. Code of conduct + removal policy.

**KPIs:** active Locals per city (≥10), walks hosted per month, accepted story suggestions per month, referral sales by Locals, NPS of walk attendees.

## 2. Referral scheme (post-launch, DTC)
**Give $20, get $20.** A friend gets $20 off their first LORE (Square/Atlas) or $10 off Pebble; the referrer gets $20 store credit (or 2 months of Explorer) once the friend's order passes the 30-day return window.

| Milestone | Bonus |
|---|---|
| 3 successful referrals | A leather lanyard |
| 5 | 12 months of LORE Pass Explorer |
| 10 | LORE Buds + a "Founding Local" invitation |

Mechanics: share from the app after a walk ends ("Send this story to a friend": the shared story page includes the referral link, which keeps it story-first). Pre-launch "skip the queue" referrals are in `kickstarter/prelaunch-plan.md`.
Fraud controls: self-referral detection (address/payment/device), credit only after the return window, and a cap of 25 rewards per year.
**Target:** 15% of new DTC orders from referrals in 2028; referral CAC ≈ $20–30 (vs $55–80 paid).

## 3. Storyteller / Creator Marketplace (concept, 2028+)

**Idea:** open LORE to vetted storytellers (licensed guides, historians, authors, museums) who publish **premium walks** and **story layers** on top of City Packs: "Caravaggio's Rome by an art historian", "Jewish Rome", "Rome after dark: crime and punishment".

| Element | Design |
|---|---|
| Who can publish | Vetted creators only: licensed guides, academics, institutions, top Locals |
| Quality bar | Same fact-card standard: each claim has a source and is reviewed by the LORE Story team (or an accredited institution partner); opinions and legends are labelled as such |
| Pricing to user | Free with Explorer for "featured" walks; premium walks €3.99–9.99 each, or included in a higher tier |
| Revenue share | 70% to the creator / 30% to LORE on premium walks (app-store-like; App Store fees apply on in-app iOS purchases) |
| Tools | A web studio: map route builder, story editor with source fields, TTS voice or the creator's own recorded voice, preview in simulation mode |
| Discovery | City page, "walks near you", ratings (verified walkers only) |
| Trust & safety | Takedown policy, corrections log, no commercial placement inside historical stories, disclosure on sponsored walks |
| B2B tie-in | Tour operators publish branded walks for their customers (`sales/b2b-playbook.md`) |

**Why it matters:** it scales content beyond our team's capacity (20 cities in 2028), turns guides from competitors into partners, and adds a revenue line.
**Validation plan:** Q1 2028 closed pilot with 10 Rome guides; success = ≥ 30% of Explorer users try a marketplace walk within 60 days, average rating ≥ 4.6.
**Risks:** quality dilution (mitigated by the review gate), guide licensing rules in Italy (Legal check: self-guided audio content is different from in-person guiding, to confirm), and payout/tax compliance (use Stripe Connect).
