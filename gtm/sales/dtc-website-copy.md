## 3. Product pages

### LORE Pebble ($149)
**H1:** LORE Pebble. The stories of the city, on a clip.
**Sub:** Our smallest LORE. No screen, one button, a soft LED ring. It uses your phone's connection, so your phone stays in your bag.
**Colour:** Bone · Ink · Terracotta. **In the box:** Pebble, clip, USB-C cable, quick-start card. 3 months of Explorer included.
**Bullets:** Clip-on ~42 × 42 × 14 mm · Map Vision (L1+L5 GNSS, 9-axis IMU) · Works offline with City Packs · 1.5-day battery (target) · Works with any Bluetooth earphones + built-in speaker · SOS, walk me home, live location (via your phone).
**Best for:** first-time travellers, students, gifting, anyone who wants LORE without a screen.
**FAQ snippets:** Needs a phone connection for live AI (offline packs work without one). Water resistance: [rating TBC by Hardware].

### LORE Square ($299)
**H1:** LORE Square. The whole city, no roaming.
**Sub:** A 1.6" AMOLED screen, a crown and button, and global data built in, in 150+ countries.* Square works on its own after setup.
**Finish:** Ink or Sand anodised aluminium. **In the box:** Square, magnetic charging dock, USB-C cable. 3 months of Explorer (1 GB/month data included).
**Bullets:** LTE Cat-4 global eSIM + Wi-Fi + Bluetooth 5.4 LE Audio · on-device 3B model for offline answers · 2-day battery (target) · Map Vision · glanceable arrows and the "look up" cue on screen · SOS with live location over its own data.
**Best for:** frequent travellers (2+ trips a year), anyone tired of roaming bills.

### LORE Atlas ($549)
**H1:** LORE Atlas. Titanium. Sapphire. Satellite SOS.
**Sub:** The same footprint as Square, made in bead-blasted or black DLC titanium with sapphire glass, a brass-tone crown, a wide-angle Glance camera and satellite SOS for when there's no network at all.**
**In the box:** Atlas, magnetic dock, leather lanyard, cable. 3 months of Explorer.
**Bullets:** LTE + eSIM + satellite SOS (NTN) · Glance camera: press and hold to ask "what am I looking at?" (LED shows when active) · on-device 3–4B model + vision · 3-day battery (target).
**Footnote:** **Satellite SOS availability depends on country and coverage. Not a replacement for emergency services.
