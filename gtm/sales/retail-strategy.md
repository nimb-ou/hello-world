# Retail Strategy (2028+)

**Gate:** no retail before DTC returns are below 5% (operating plan, "What we will NOT do"). Retail begins with a curated design-store pilot in H1 2028, then airport tech in H2 2028, then electronics chains in 2029 (if sell-through supports it).
All retailers named are **prospect categories/targets**. There are no agreements.

## 1. Why retail, and which retail
| Channel | Why | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|
| **Design & concept stores** | Brand halo, the "quiet luxury" positioning, gifting | Low volume; consignment terms |
| **Airport travel-tech** | The highest-intent moment: people buy travel gadgets *while travelling* | High rent/margin demands, slotting fees, demo needs |
| **Travel/outdoor retailers** | Gear-aware shoppers (P1, P2) | Seasonal |
| **Electronics chains** | Scale | Price pressure, returns, heavy marketing support |
| **Museum shops (Rome, then other cities)** | Story fit, City bundles | Small volumes; buying cycles |

## 3. Commercial terms (planning assumptions, est.)
| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Wholesale discount (design/specialty) | 40–45% off MSRP |
| Airport retail | 45–50% off MSRP, or consignment + co-op marketing 3–5% |
| Electronics chains | 30–35% margin + MDF 3–5% + return allowance |
| MAP (minimum advertised price) policy | Yes, from day 1 |
| Payment terms | Net 30 (specialty), Net 60 (chains) |
| Display | Countertop demo stand with a looping 0:30 video, a tethered Pebble/Square, and earbuds + hygiene covers |
| Training | 20-minute staff module + demo script |

## 4. Rollout
| Phase | Doors | KPI to proceed |
|---|---|---|
| H1 2028 pilot | 5 design stores + 2 Rome museum shops | Sell-through ≥ 3 units/door/week; returns < 5% |
| H2 2028 | +1 airport retailer pilot (10 doors in hubs: JFK, LHR, FCO, LIS, CDG) | ≥ 6 units/door/week; Square ≥ 40% of mix |
| 2029 | Electronics chain in 1 country | Retail contribution margin ≥ 20% after MDF |

## 5. Retail pitch (one-liner)
"LORE replaces the travel eSIM, the audio guide and the power bank in a single 54 mm object, and it's bought at the exact moment people are travelling."
