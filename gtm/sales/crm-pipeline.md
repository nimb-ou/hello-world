# CRM Pipeline Stages

Tool: HubSpot (Starter → Pro once there are 2+ reps). Pipelines: **B2B Fleet**, **Retail/Wholesale**, **Partnerships**, **Creators**. Consumer contacts live in the ESP (Klaviyo) and sync to HubSpot as lifecycle stages only.

## 1. B2B Fleet pipeline
| # | Stage | Entry criteria | Exit criteria | Probability | Target days in stage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Target | ICP fit (segment, city with a City Pack, ≥40 beds / ≥5k guests a year) | Contact identified | 0% | — |
| 1 | Contacted | First touch sent (email/LinkedIn/event) | Reply or meeting booked | 2% | 14 |
| 2 | Discovery | Meeting held; pain + volume known | Qualified on BANT-lite (budget line, decision maker, need, start date) | 10% | 14 |
| 3 | Demo / walk | On-site or remote walk demo done | Pilot proposal requested | 25% | 14 |
| 4 | Pilot proposal | Proposal sent with success criteria | Pilot agreement signed | 40% | 21 |
| 5 | Pilot live | Devices delivered | Pilot report delivered | 60% | 60 |
| 6 | Commercial negotiation | Pilot met ≥2 of 3 success criteria | Contract signed | 80% | 21 |
| 7 | Closed won | Signed annual / seasonal | — | 100% | — |
| 8 | Closed lost | Reason recorded (price, hygiene, priority, competitor, no response) | Nurture in 6 months | 0% | — |

Required fields: segment, city, beds/guests per year, devices est., monthly value, source, pilot start/end, success metrics, loss reason.

## 2. Retail/Wholesale pipeline
Prospect → Buyer contacted → Samples sent → Line review scheduled → Terms negotiated → PO received → Live in store → Reorder (sell-through ≥ target) / Delisted.

## 3. Partnerships pipeline
Identified → Intro → Value exchange defined → Legal/contract → Live → Quarterly review.

## 4. Creators pipeline
Prospect (vetted) → Outreach → Interested → Rates agreed → Contract signed → Product shipped → Content approved (facts) → Live → Reported (scorecard) → Rebook / End.

## 5. Consumer lifecycle stages (synced)
Subscriber (waitlist) → Hot lead (VIP $1 / KS follower / 2+ clicks) → Backer → Customer (DTC) → Activated (first walk) → Subscriber (paid Pass) → Advocate (referral/review/LORE Local) → Churn risk.

## 6. Hygiene rules
Next step + date is mandatory on every open deal; deals with no activity for 30 days are auto-flagged; weekly pipeline review on Monday; and no deal reaches Stage 4 without a written success-criteria field.
