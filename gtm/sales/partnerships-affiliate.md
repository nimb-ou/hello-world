# Partnerships & Affiliate Plan

Two directions: **(A) outbound affiliate revenue**, where LORE earns commission when users book hotels, tours or restaurants through the app (a COMPANY.md revenue line); and **(B) inbound affiliates**, where publishers and creators earn commission for selling LORE.
No partnerships exist today. All programmes are to be applied for.

## A. Booking affiliate revenue (LORE earns)

| Programme | What | Typical commission (sourced) | Cookie | How LORE uses it | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **GetYourGuide Partner Program** | Tours, tickets | ~8% of booking value (base)¹ | 31 days (reported) | "Skip the line for the Colosseum?" offered only after a story, never pushy | Some sources cite 7% via CJ or 2–5%: confirm on signup |
| **Viator Partner Program** (Tripadvisor) | Tours, tickets | Up to 8%¹ | 30 days | Same; A/B test vs GYG by city | Older sources cite 3% for the US: confirm |
| **Booking.com Affiliate Partner Programme** | Hotels | Share of Booking.com's commission (reported 25–40% of its commission, tiered by volume), *not* a % of the room rate² | Session-based (reported) | "Need a place to stay tonight nearby?" in the app | Programme structure is reported to have changed (CJ routing): verify |
| **Expedia Group / Hotels.com** (via Expedia Creator/Affiliate) | Hotels | ~2–6% (reported; varies by product) | 7 days (reported) | Alternate supply | Verify |
| **TheFork** (Tripadvisor) / OpenTable | Restaurant bookings | Per-cover fees; affiliate availability varies by market | — | "Book this trattoria for 20:00" | Partnership BD, not self-serve |
| **Airalo-type eSIMs** | Only for Pebble users (no eSIM) | ~10% (typical; verify) | — | Possibly conflicts with LORE Pass data; deprioritise | |

**Revenue model (est.):** at 10k active users in 2028, 8% making one €60 tour booking per trip at 8% → ~€3.8k per trip cycle; hotel bookings add more. Affiliate revenue is a *nice to have* with a trust cap.

**Trust rules:** (1) Story first, commerce second: never inside a historical story. (2) Disclose "LORE may earn a commission" in the UI. (3) Recommendations are ranked by quality signals, never by commission. (4) Users can switch off suggestions.
