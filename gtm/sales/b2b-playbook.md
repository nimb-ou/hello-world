# B2B Sales Playbook: Fleet Rental

Segments: **hostels & boutique hotels**, **tour operators / walking-tour companies**, **cruise lines (shore excursions)**, **corporate travel / events (MICE)**, and later **universities (study abroad)**.
Product: **LORE Fleet**, a Pebble (or Square) fleet + multi-dock charging tray + fleet dashboard + optional branded stories.
Status: no customers yet. Pilots start Q4 2027 (operating plan Stage 3). Everything below is a plan.

## 1. Market signals
- Rome: **22.9M arrivals in 2025** (record, Lazio tourism board via Wanted in Rome).¹
- Civitavecchia (Rome's cruise port): **~3.56M cruise passengers in 2025**, Italy's busiest cruise port.²
- Group audio systems (whisper headsets) typically rent at around **€2/person/day** (e.g. a Sarajevo operator) or per-unit quotes; the global tour-guide-headset market is estimated at ~$360M (market-research claim).³ LORE is self-guided, which is a different product from guide-led audio, but it anchors willingness to pay.

## 2. Value proposition by segment
| Segment | Buyer | Pain | LORE pitch | Revenue model for them |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hostels / hotels | GM, guest-experience lead | Guests ask the desk "what should I see?"; a commoditised stay | "Give every guest a local friend." Rent at the desk or include in premium rooms; branded welcome story | €8–12/day rental to guests, keep 60–70% |
| Walking-tour operators | Ops/product manager | Guide capacity, off-peak, languages | Self-guided "LORE edition" tours sold in the off-season or as an add-on; multilingual at once | New SKU at €15–25/person |
| Cruise lines | Shore-excursion manager | 8-hour port calls, guests nervous going solo; tender timing | "Independent Rome, with a safety net": walk-me-home to the meeting point, live location, return-time alerts | Sold as a shore excursion at $39–59/guest |
| Corporate travel / MICE | Travel/event manager | Delegates' free evenings, duty of care | Duty-of-care features (location sharing opt-in, SOS) + a curated evening walk | Per-delegate fee in the event budget |
| Universities | Study-abroad office | Student safety and orientation | Semester rental + safety features | Programme fee line |

## 3. Pricing (planning, est.; Finance to validate)
| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Fleet Rent** (per device) | **$19/device/month** (12-month term), min 10 devices | Pebble, data via Wi-Fi/phone tethering *or* Square at $34/device/month with data (fair use 1 GB/device/month), dashboard, replacement of faulty units, all City Packs |
| **Fleet Seasonal** | $29/device/month, 4-month minimum (Apr–Oct) | Same |
| **Revenue share** (cruise/tours) | $0 hardware + 30% of the guest-facing price, min $6/guest-day | For large operators |
| **Branded stories** | $1,500 setup + $250/month | Up to 20 custom fact-checked stories (e.g. the hostel's building history) |
| **Hygiene kit** | $2/guest (single-use ear covers if loaner earbuds are supplied) | Optional |
| Loss/damage | Loss covered up to 5% of the fleet per year; beyond that $99 Pebble / $199 Square | |

Example (hostel, 20 Pebbles, rented to guests at €10/day at 35% utilisation): ~2,555 rental days/yr × €10 = €25.5k revenue vs fleet cost of 20 × $19 × 12 = $4,560/yr → strong ROI. *Utilisation is an assumption to be measured in pilots.*

## 4. Pilot terms (standard)
- **Duration:** 60 days, 10–30 devices.
- **Price:** free hardware loan; the customer pays $0 for the first 30 days, then a 50% discount for days 31–60; converts to an annual plan unless cancelled.
- **Success criteria (agreed up front):** utilisation ≥ 25% of guest-days, guest CSAT ≥ 4.5/5, ≥ 10% of renters opt in to email (lead gen for DTC), and the operator NPS.
- **Our obligations:** onboarding + staff training (60 min), weekly report, 48h device replacement in the EU.
- **Their obligations:** desk placement + signage, staff pitch at check-in, data sharing (anonymised usage), a case-study right *if* successful (written approval required).
- **Target pilots (5) by Q1 2028:** 2 hostels (Rome, Lisbon), 2 walking-tour operators (Rome), 1 cruise-excursion partner (Civitavecchia call).

## 5. Pitch narrative (10-minute discovery → demo)
1. **Hook:** "What do your guests ask your front desk most often?"
2. **Problem:** guests want local, true stories and safety, while staff time is limited.
3. **Demo:** a 5-minute walk from their door with a LORE (we preload a branded welcome story).
4. **Economics:** the utilisation calculator (spreadsheet) with their numbers.
5. **Risk reversal:** a 60-day pilot, no hardware cost.
6. **Close:** agree success criteria + start date.

Prospecting sources: ITB Berlin (March), WTM London (November), Hostelworld's top-rated property lists, Viator/GetYourGuide top-rated operators in Rome, cruise-line shore-excursion procurement contacts (via Seatrade Cruise Global, April), and LinkedIn Sales Navigator (titles: Head of Guest Experience, Shore Excursions Manager, Travel Manager).

## 6. Objection handling
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Guests have phones." | Phones drain, roam and get snatched. LORE gives a curated, sourced experience the phone doesn't, and frees guests' phones for maps and payments. |
| "Hygiene of shared devices." | Guests use their own earbuds; the device is wipeable PC/glass fibre; optional single-use covers for loaner buds; a UV-C box is an option on the multi-dock tray [Hardware to confirm]. |
| "Loss and theft." | Location tracking on every fleet device, a deposit option, and a loss allowance in the contract. |
| "Staff don't have time." | Self-serve QR checkout on the dashboard; 60-minute training; we handle replacements. |
| "We already sell walking tours." | LORE sells the hours your guides aren't working: evenings, off-season, languages. Add a branded "with [your company]" edition. |
| "It's unproven." | 60-day pilot, agreed success metrics, no hardware cost. |
| "Data privacy (GDPR)." | Location is processed on the device; fleet dashboards show aggregate usage only; a DPA is provided (Legal). |
| "Price." | Show the per-guest-day economics: at 25% utilisation the fleet costs < €1 per available guest-night. |
