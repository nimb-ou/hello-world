## 2. Valuation framework for LORE: sum of the parts on forward revenue

| Revenue type | Multiple range (forward) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hardware | 1.5-3x | GoPro, Nothing |
| Subscription ARR (Explorer) | 6-10x | Oura and Whoop sit at the top. Discounted for optional (not mandatory) attach and ~6% monthly churn |
| Affiliate, B2B fleet, accessories | 2-4x | |

**At the Series A (Sep-2028), base case:**

| Metric (model) | Value |
|---|---|
| FY2028 revenue | $10.1M (Aug-28 month $0.88M ≈ $10.6M run-rate; Dec-28 month $1.9M) |
| FY2029 forecast revenue | $31.6M (hardware $26.9M, recurring + other $4.8M) |
| Subscription ARR end-2028 / end-2029 | $0.8M / $3.3M |
| Blended GM FY28 → FY29 | 27% → 33% |

Forward value: FY29 hardware $26.9M × 2.5 ≈ $67M, ARR $3.3M × 8 ≈ $27M, other $2.5M × 3 ≈ $7M → **≈ $100M.** A **$105M post-money** is therefore supportable only if investors underwrite FY2029 (3.3x forward revenue, ≈ 10x run-rate). That is a stretch for hardware, and it relies on the subscription and AI story.

## 3. What has to be true for ≥ $100M (Series A scorecard)

| Metric | Required at Series A | Base model | Bull |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue run-rate | ≥ $10M (≥ $20M after Q4-28) | ≈ $10.6M (Aug-28) | ≈ $15M |
| Forward-year revenue growth | ≥ 3x | 3.1x | 3.2x |
| Paid subscribers / attach | ≥ 7k paid, trial→paid ≥ 35% | 7.7k / 35% | 9k+ / 44% |
| Monthly churn (cohort months 2-6) | ≤ 6% | 6% | 4.8% |
| Hardware GM trajectory | ≥ 25% now, path to ≥ 35% | 25% → 34% (FY31) | higher |
| Square landed (D11) | ≤ $185 ex-NRE | $184 | |
| Cities live | 20 | 20 | |
| CAC (DTC paid) | ≤ $60, ≥ 30% organic/referral | $60 | $51 |
| Return rate | ≤ 4% | 4% | |

**If the base metrics land but the market applies pure hardware multiples (2-3x)**, the Series A prices at **$60-75M pre**. The model's founder stake after A would then fall from 37.7% to roughly 35-36%. The company still clears **$100M+ by the Series B (2030)**: FY2030 revenue $67M at ≥ 2x hardware, plus $7.6M ARR at 8x, gives ≈ $170-250M. The plan assumes $250M post.

## 4. Path to $100M without heroic multiples

1. **FY2029 revenue ≥ $30M at ≥ 33% GM** (base: $31.6M / 33%) → 3x forward ≈ $95-100M by early 2029.
2. **Subscription ARR ≥ $5M** (base reaches this in H1-2030). Each $1M of ARR adds ~$8M of value, and raising attach from 35% to 45% speeds this up by ~6 months.
3. **EBITDA visibility**: base EBITDA is positive in FY2031 (+$0.8M). Hitting the D11 Square target pulls it to +$3.0M, and Square at $329 to +$4.4M.
4. **Avoid the Humane trap**: do not raise above what the next round's metrics can justify. A flat or down round on a hardware company is far more damaging than a smaller A.
