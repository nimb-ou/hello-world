# LORE: Customer Survey, Interview Script and Validation Plan

*LORE Labs Market Research. Prepared 30 Oct 2026. Ready to run on Typeform/Qualtrics (survey) and Zoom/in-person (interviews). Fieldwork: Nov 2026 – Jan 2027, before the 50-unit EVT beta (Q1 2027) and the Kickstarter (Q2 2027).*

---

## Part A: Quantitative survey (25 questions, ~9 minutes)

**Sample:** n = 600 completes (US 250, UK 150, EU 200 across DE/FR/NL/ES/IT), via Prolific or Cint panel, plus a separate open-link sample from the LORE waitlist (analysed separately to avoid enthusiast bias).
**Quotas:** age 22–70; ≥50% have taken 2+ international trips in the last 24 months; ≥40% have taken a solo international trip in the last 24 months; gender balanced.
**Incentive:** panel standard (~$2–3) for panel respondents; a prize draw (1 × Square) for the waitlist sample.
**Randomisation:** Q17–Q20 (Van Westendorp) are shown for one randomly assigned SKU per respondent (Pebble 35%, Square 40%, Atlas 25%). Q21 uses monadic price cells.

### Screener

**Q1.** How many international trips (leisure or business, at least one night abroad) have you taken in the last 24 months?
◯ 0 *(terminate)* ◯ 1 ◯ 2–3 ◯ 4–6 ◯ 7+

**Q2.** Of those trips, how many did you take **alone** (no travel companion for most of the trip)?
◯ None ◯ 1 ◯ 2–3 ◯ 4+

**Q3.** Age band: ◯ 18–21 ◯ 22–29 ◯ 30–37 ◯ 38–45 ◯ 46–55 ◯ 56–65 ◯ 66+

**Q4.** Country of residence *(drop-down)*, plus annual household income band (local-currency bands).

### Trip behaviour

**Q5.** On your last city trip abroad, how did you mainly find out about the places you were walking past? *(multi-select)*
☐ Guidebook ☐ Google/Apple Maps ☐ Wikipedia/web search on phone ☐ ChatGPT/Gemini or another AI ☐ Audio-guide app (e.g., Rick Steves, VoiceMap) ☐ Paid guided/walking tour ☐ Free walking tour ☐ Museum audio guide ☐ I mostly didn't ☐ Other

**Q6.** How did you get mobile data on your last trip outside your home region?
◯ Home carrier roaming (day pass) ◯ Travel eSIM (Airalo, Holafly, Saily…) ◯ Local physical SIM ◯ Wi-Fi only ◯ Don't remember

**Q7.** Roughly how much did you spend on mobile data for that trip? *(numeric, local currency)*

**Q8.** During a typical day of sightseeing abroad, how often does your phone battery drop below 20% before you get back to your accommodation?
◯ Never ◯ Rarely ◯ About half the days ◯ Most days ◯ Every day

**Q9.** How much do you agree? *(5-point scale, randomised order)*
a) "I spend too much time looking at my phone when I explore a new city."
b) "I worry about my phone being stolen when I use it on the street abroad."
c) "I'd like to know the history and stories of the places I walk past."
d) "I don't trust AI to give me accurate historical facts."
e) "I feel less safe walking alone at night in an unfamiliar city."
f) "I'd like someone at home to know where I am when I'm out alone abroad."

**Q10.** Have you paid for any of the following in the last 24 months? *(multi-select)*
☐ Guided city walking tour ☐ Audio-guide app or tour ☐ Museum audio guide ☐ Travel eSIM ☐ Personal safety device or app subscription ☐ Translation device ☐ None

**Q11.** What is the **single biggest frustration** when you explore a new city on foot? *(open text)*

### Concept exposure

*[Show the 30-second concept video plus a three-SKU spec card: Pebble / Square / Atlas, prices hidden at this stage.]*

**Q12.** How appealing is this concept to you overall? ◯ 1 Not at all … ◯ 5 Extremely

**Q13.** Which three features matter most to you? *(choose up to 3; randomised)*
☐ Stories about the buildings around me ("on your right…") ☐ Facts are verified with sources ☐ Works offline ☐ Built-in global data, no roaming bill ☐ 2–3 day battery, saves my phone battery ☐ Phone stays in my pocket ☐ SOS button and live location sharing ☐ "Walk me home" check-ins ☐ Restaurant/stay recommendations ☐ Uses my own earphones ☐ Translation ☐ Satellite SOS (no signal needed)

**Q14.** Which would you **most** likely use for this? ◯ A dedicated device like this ◯ An app on my phone ◯ Smart glasses (e.g., Ray-Ban Meta) ◯ None of these

**Q15.** What, if anything, would stop you from buying this? *(multi-select + other)*
☐ My phone already does this ☐ Another device to carry and charge ☐ Price ☐ Subscription ☐ Doubt the stories are accurate or interesting ☐ Privacy / location data ☐ Looks ☐ I don't travel enough ☐ Other

**Q16.** How would you prefer to pay for live AI + data + safety?
◯ Monthly subscription ◯ Annual subscription ◯ Per-trip pass ◯ One-off higher device price, no subscription ◯ Free version only

### Pricing (Van Westendorp for one assigned SKU; open numeric, local currency)

**Q17.** At what price would you consider **[SKU]** so expensive that you would not consider buying it?
**Q18.** At what price would you consider it priced so low that you would question its quality?
**Q19.** At what price would it start to feel expensive, so that it's not out of the question but you'd have to think about it?
**Q20.** At what price would it be a bargain, a great buy for the money?

**Q21.** *(Monadic price cell, random)* If **[SKU]** cost **[price]**, including 3 months of the Explorer plan, how likely would you be to buy one in the next 12 months?
◯ Definitely would ◯ Probably would ◯ Might or might not ◯ Probably not ◯ Definitely not
*Cells: Pebble $129/$149/$169 · Square $249/$299/$349 · Atlas $499/$549/$599/$649*

**Q22.** After the included months, which plan would you most likely choose?
◯ Explorer $9.99/month ◯ Explorer $79/year ◯ Trip Pass $29 per 14-day trip ◯ Free tier only ◯ Wouldn't keep using it

### Context and follow-up

**Q23.** Who would you consider buying this for? *(multi-select)* ☐ Myself ☐ Partner ☐ Parent(s) ☐ Child/student ☐ Friend ☐ Nobody

**Q24.** Which city would you most like LORE to cover first? *(drop-down of 15 candidate cities + other)*

**Q25.** May we contact you for a 30-minute paid interview ($50/£40/€45 voucher) or a beta test? ◯ Yes (email) ◯ No

### Analysis plan

- Weight to the quota targets. Report top-2-box (T2B) purchase intent, calibrated with a standard haircut (Definitely × 0.7 + Probably × 0.3) to estimate the realistic purchase rate.
- Van Westendorp per SKU: PMC, OPP, IPP and PME, cut by persona proxy (Q2 solo count × Q3 age × Q9e safety).
- TURF analysis on Q13 to choose the hero message mix.
- Driver analysis (Q9/Q13 → Q12 and Q21).
- Q11/Q15 open-text coded into themes (two coders, κ > 0.7).
- Q24: city demand ranking, which feeds [launch-markets.md](launch-markets.md).

## Part B: Interview script (20 interviews × 30–40 minutes)

**Recruitment (20):** 6 × Maya-type (solo culture, 22–45), 3 × Arjun-type (frequent budget hopper), 4 × Claire-type (solo women, safety-led), 3 × retirees (one couple interviewed together), 2 × business travellers, 2 × study-abroad students or their parents. Recruit from survey Q25, solo-travel communities (with moderator permission) and the LORE waitlist (max 5 from the waitlist). Spread: 8 US, 6 UK, 6 EU. At least 8 interviews must be with people who travelled abroad in the last 60 days.

**Rules:** Mom Test principles: ask about past behaviour, not hypotheticals, until Part 4. No pitching before Part 4. Record with consent (GDPR consent form, retention 12 months). Two people per interview (an interviewer and a note-taker). Write a synthesis within 24 hours.

### Part 1: Warm-up (3 min)
1. Tell me about the last trip abroad you took. Where, how long, who with?
2. How did you decide where to go?

### Part 2: The last city walk (10 min)
3. Take me through one specific day of walking around that city, from leaving your accommodation. What did you do first?
4. How did you know where to go next? What did you look at?
5. Was there a moment when you were looking at something and wondered what it was? What did you do then?
6. How often did you take your phone out? Any moment you felt uncomfortable doing so?
7. When did your phone battery become an issue, if ever?
8. How did you get data? What did it cost? Any surprises?
9. Did you use any audio guide, tour, app or AI that day? What was good or bad about it?

### Part 3: Safety and solo (7 min)
10. Was there a time on that trip, or another one, when you felt unsafe or uneasy? What happened? What did you do?
11. Who knows where you are when you travel? How do they know?
12. Have you ever had something stolen while travelling? Tell me about it.
13. What do you do *today* to feel safer? Do you pay for anything?

### Part 4: Concept reaction (10 min)
*[Show the concept video, then hand over a Pebble/Square 3D-printed dummy or render, then play a 40-second sample story on headphones (Rome, via the prototype PWA).]*
14. In your own words, what is this?
15. Where in the day you described would this have helped, if anywhere?
16. What's the first thing you'd worry about?
17. Which of the three would you pick, and why? *(show prices only now)*
18. What would you expect to pay? What feels too expensive?
19. How would you feel about a subscription after 3 months? Which plan?
20. If you had it on your last trip, what would you have *stopped* doing or paying for?

### Part 5: Close (3 min)
21. Who else do you know who should have this?
22. If we offered a beta unit for your next trip in exchange for feedback, would you want it? When is your next trip?
23. Anything I didn't ask that I should have?

**Synthesis template (one per interview):** persona fit · trip context · top 3 pains (with verbatim quotes, marked as real) · current workaround and its cost · concept reaction (−2 to +2) · SKU chosen · WTP stated · subscription preference · objections · beta interest (Y/N, next trip date).

## Part C: Validation plan and success thresholds

| # | Hypothesis | Method | **Pass threshold (go)** | Kill / pivot threshold | Owner / date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Solo/independent frequent travellers have a strong "understand the place, eyes up" need | Q9a/c T2B; interviews Q5–6 | ≥60% T2B on Q9c **and** ≥40% on Q9a; ≥12 of 20 interviewees describe a "wondered what it was" moment unprompted | <40% on Q9c | Research, Dec 2026 |
| H2 | The concept appeals to the target | Q12 T2B (target sample) | **≥35% T2B** (benchmark for a good new-to-world hardware concept) | <20% | Research, Dec 2026 |
| H3 | Device beats app for enough people | Q14 | **≥25%** choose "dedicated device" (target sample) | <12%, which means pivot to app-first and Pebble only | Research, Dec 2026 |
| H4 | Prices sit in the acceptable range | Van Westendorp Q17–20 | Pebble $149, Square $299, Atlas $599 each inside PMC–PME for the primary persona; Atlas IPP ≥ $520 | Any list price above the PME → reprice | Research + Finance, Jan 2027 |
| H5 | Real purchase intent at list price | Q21 calibrated | Calibrated purchase ≥ **8%** for Square at $299 in the target sample; Atlas $599 within 20% (relative) of the $549 cell | <4% | Research, Jan 2027 |
| H6 | The subscription model is acceptable | Q16, Q22 | ≥45% choose a paid option (Explorer monthly/annual or Trip Pass) | >60% "free only" or "wouldn't keep using" | Research, Jan 2027 |
| H7 | Verified facts are a differentiator | Q9d, Q13 | "Facts are verified" in the top-4 Q13 features; ≥40% agree with Q9d | Not in the top 6 | Research, Dec 2026 |
| H8 | Safety is a purchase justifier for women and parents | Q9e/f by gender; Q23 | ≥55% T2B on Q9f among women; ≥15% would buy for a child or parent | | Research, Dec 2026 |
| H9 | Rome is the right City #1 | Q24 + launch-markets scoring | Rome in the top 3 of Q24 among US/UK respondents | Rome below #5 → revisit | Research, Jan 2027 |
| H10 | Waitlist demand supports a Kickstarter | Landing page + deposits | **≥25,000 emails and ≥2,500 paid $5 deposits** by Kickstarter launch; email→deposit ≥10% | <8,000 emails by T−6 weeks → delay the campaign | GTM, Q2 2027 |
| H11 | Beta users actually use it | 50 EVT units, trip diaries | ≥70% of beta users use it on ≥60% of trip days; NPS ≥ 40; ≥3 stories listened to per hour walked | <40% usage → fix the core loop before Kickstarter | Product + Research, Q1 2027 |

**Decision gate (end Jan 2027):** proceed to Kickstarter at the recommended prices if **H2, H3, H4 and H5 pass and none of H1–H8 hits a kill threshold**. If H3 fails but H2 passes, re-sequence: launch the app + Pebble first and move Square/Atlas to 2028.
