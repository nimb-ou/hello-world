## 1. Record travel volumes

- **1.52B international arrivals in 2025 (+4%)**, close to the 2009–2019 average growth rate. Europe had 793M (+4%, +6% vs 2019) and Asia-Pacific 331M (+6%, still 9% below 2019). UN Tourism forecasts **+3–4% in 2026**. https://www.untourism.int/news/international-tourist-arrivals-up-4-in-2025-reflecting-strong-travel-demand-around-the-world
- International tourism receipts were about $1.9T in 2025, and total tourism exports $2.2T. https://mexicobusiness.news/trade-and-investment/news/2025-global-tourist-arrivals-4-receipts-hit-us19-trillion
- The top 100 city destinations received 702M international arrivals (+8%); Bangkok led with 30.3M. https://www.travelmole.com/news/euromonitor-international-top-100-city-destinations-2025
- France reached a record 102M international tourists in 2025. https://euronews.com/travel/2026/02/20/france-welcomed-record-visitor-numbers-in-2025-retaining-its-title-as-worlds-most-visited-

**Implication:** city-break volume is growing faster than global arrivals (+8% vs +4%). That is LORE's use case.

## 2. Solo travel is mainstream

- Solo travel market estimates: **$482B (2024) → ~$1.07T (2030), ~14.3% CAGR** (TechSci/Grand View). https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/05/06/3074793/0/en/Solo-Travel-Market-to-Hit-USD-1-07-Trillion-by-2030-Fueled-by-Gen-Z-and-Millennial-Wanderlust.html
- 43% of Skyscanner respondents travelled solo in the past year. https://www.skyscanner.com/tips-and-inspiration/solo-travel-statistics
- 48% of IMG respondents planned a solo trip in 2025. https://www.itij.com/latest/news/img-travel-outlook-survey-predicts-top-destinations-and-travel-trends-2025
- Solo share of Hostelworld bookings rose from 57% (2021) to 63% (2024), with solo female backpackers driving the shift. https://www.lbc.co.uk/business/695e3eaacae74c418a1ed0ac93bc787f
- Gen Z and millennials lead solo travel. https://www.thestar.com.my/lifestyle/travel/2025/09/08/gen-z-and-millennial-travellers-lead-the-way-in-solo-travels ; in India, Gen Z and millennials made 9 in 10 international trips in 2025. https://www.theweek.in/news/biz-tech/2025/12/19/90-pc-international-trips-from-india-are-by-gen-z-milennials-travel-report.amp.html
- Solo-travel search interest hit a record high in 2026 (Axios, Aug 2026). https://www.axios.com/local/richmond/2026/08/17/solo-travel-searches-hit-a-record-high-in-2026

**Implication:** the primary persona is large and growing. The solo traveller has no companion to share discovery or watch their back, and LORE's "local friend" positioning maps directly onto that gap.

## 3. AI from planning to in-trip

- **Phocuswright:** 33% of US travellers use GenAI somewhere in the travel process (UK 22%, France 19%, Germany 15%), and 78% say it improves planning. https://www.phocuswright.com/Travel-Research/Research-Updates/2025/From-hype-to-habit-78-percent-of-travelers-say-GenAI-improves-trip-planning
- **Adobe:** 29% of US consumers have used AI to plan trips. https://business.adobe.com/blog/consumers-embrace-generative-ai-for-trip-planning
- **Global Rescue (Fall 2025):** 22% have used GenAI to plan a trip (non-US 30%). https://www.globalrescue.com/common/blog/detail/ai-agentic-trip-planning/
- McKinsey charts the rise of "agentic AI" in travel booking. https://www.mckinsey.com/featured-insights/charts/agentic-ai-adventures
- A wave of AI audio-guide apps (Audiala, Guidude, PodWalk, CityTour AI, Hear2GO, Waytale, Zigway) launched in 2025–2026 (see [competitors.md](competitors.md)).

**Implication:** the 2027 shift is from "AI plans my trip" to "AI is with me on my trip." LORE's timing is right, but the "LLM narrates POIs" layer is commoditising. LORE must compete on **trust (verified facts)** and **form factor**.

## 4. AI wearables go mass-market

- EssilorLuxottica sold **~7M Ray-Ban/Oakley Meta smart glasses in 2025**, more than triple 2024, and has talked about capacity of 10M+ a year by end-2026. https://uploadvr.com/meta-essilorluxottica-sold-7-million-smart-glasses-in-2025/
- Meta Ray-Ban Display launched at $799. https://parameter.io/meta-stock-company-launches-799-ray-ban-smart-glasses-with-display
- Google/Samsung Gemini glasses (Warby Parker, Gentle Monster) launch in select markets in fall 2026. https://mobilesyrup.com/2026/05/19/samsung-reveals-warby-parker-gentle-monster-android-xr-smartglasses/
- OpenAI × io device: a screenless pocket device expected H2 2026 to 2027. https://introl.com/blog/openai-consumer-device-jony-ive-hardware-2026
- Consolidation: Meta acquired Limitless (Dec 2025), Amazon acquired Bee (Jul 2025), and HP acquired Humane's assets ($116M, Feb 2025).
- The AI wearables market is $30–72B in 2025 depending on definition (see [market-sizing.md](market-sizing.md)).

**Implication:** consumers will arrive in 2027 already primed for "AI you wear," which lowers LORE's education cost. Big Tech captures the general assistant job, so LORE must own the travel job and stay interoperable (LORE audio through any earbuds or glasses).

## 5. Travel eSIM displaces roaming

- Travel eSIM revenue was **$989M (2024), ~$1.8B (2025, +85%), and >$8.7B by 2030** (Juniper). Operators are expected to launch their own travel eSIMs from 2026. https://www.telecoms.com/esim/travel-esim-market-forecasted-to-grow-85-this-year
- eSIM-capable smartphones: 2.8B (2025) to 5.6B (2030).
- Airalo became a unicorn with 20M+ users. https://www.airalo.com/about-us/press-releases/airalo-becomes-first-esim-unicorn
- Bill shock persists: in one frequent-traveller survey, over 70% had experienced bill shock, averaging over €200. https://telecompaper.com/news/half-of-travellers-ok-with-higher-cost-for-roaming--1067256

**Implication:** "no roaming bill" is a hygiene feature with falling perceived value, as eSIM data trends towards $2–4/GB retail. Square/Atlas data matters because **the device works alone** (phone stolen, phone dead), not because it is cheap data.

## 6. Overtourism and the "better visitor"

- Venice's day-tripper fee expanded to 54 days in 2025 and 60 days in 2026 (€5–10). Barcelona's tourist tax rises toward €8/night by 2029, and the city is cutting cruise terminals from 7 to 5. Kyoto's lodging tax rose to as much as ¥10,000/night from March 2026. https://www.fortune.com/europe/2025/01/15/city-visitor-hotspots-fighting-back-overtourism ; https://en.10minhotel.com/2025/07/31/overtourism-when-destinations-play-the-tax-is-right ; https://www.travelpirates.com/captains-log/places-to-avoid-summer-2026
- Anti-tourism protests across Southern Europe in summer 2025. https://kongres-magazine.eu/2025/07/overtourism-reaches-breaking-point-as-europeans-take-to-the-streets/
- Rome's Jubilee brought a record ~33.5M pilgrims in 2025, but sites under scaffolding left some visitors short-changed. https://www.ksl.com/article/51428341/rome-welcomed-record-335-million-pilgrims-for-catholic-holy-year

**Implication:** LORE can route people **away from the hotspots** ("hidden Rome" walks, crowd-aware suggestions), which is a story for destination management organisations (DMOs) and potential tourism-board B2B deals (SmartGuide already sells to DMOs at €190+/month). Messaging: *"be a better visitor."*

## 7. Digital detox / phone-light travel

- 72% of US TripIt users want to unplug from phones and social media while travelling. https://www.tripit.com/web/blog/travel-tips/wellness-travel-trends-2025
- Hilton 2025 trends: 17% of vacationers seek destinations that force them to unplug. https://traveltomorrow.com/10-travel-trends-for-2025-according-to-hilton/
- In Australia, 76% of 18–25-year-olds are willing to do a summer digital detox (TTF survey). https://www.empower.com/the-currency/play/beyond-wi-fi-signal-digital-detox-vacations-news
- The Global Wellness Summit describes the shift as "not just digital detox, but analog travel." https://www.globalwellnesssummit.com/?p=53423

**Implication:** LORE's "eyes up, phone in pocket" message rides this trend, and it is unusual for a tech product to fit it. **Do not** frame LORE as "another screen." Pebble has no screen, and Square/Atlas screens should be glance-only.

## 8. Safety

- 66% of women in one solo-female-traveller survey worry about personal safety. https://citynews.com.au/2025/womens-safety-premium-for-solo-travel-peace-of-mind/
- Global Rescue: women take significantly more safety precautions, and nearly half avoid certain countries when travelling solo. https://www.globalrescue.com/common/blog/detail/travel-trends-reveal-key-gender-differences/
- Reported harassment of women travellers rose 60% (ITIJ). https://www.itij.com/latest/news/womens-travel-safety-concerns-grow-harassment-reports-surge-60
- Pickpocketing: Greater Rome had 33,455 incidents in 2024 (+68% vs 2019), and Barcelona about 239 thefts a day in 2025 (secondary aggregation). https://www.quotezone.co.uk/travel-insurance/guides/pickpocketing-statistics-in-europe
- Apple keeps satellite SOS free on iPhone 14/15 and gives 2 years free on Watch Ultra 3. https://appleinsider.com/articles/25/09/09/iphone-14-and-iphone-15-get-an-extra-year-of-satellite-features-for-free

**Implication:** safety is a strong *purchase justifier* (women, parents of students, retirees). Satellite SOS itself is being commoditised by Apple, so LORE's safety value is **phone-independent location sharing, discreet SOS, and "walk me home" check-ins**.

## 9. Personalisation ("Era of You")

- Booking.com's 2026 predictions (29,000 travellers, 33 markets) centre on individuality: Destined-ations, Romantasy retreats, "Hushed Hobbies" (25% say quieter hobbies alone justify a trip), and tech-assisted wellness. https://www.ttgasia.com/2025/11/24/travel-in-2026-to-focus-on-individuality-booking-com-predicts/
- Mastercard Economics Institute 2025: travel is "purpose-driven" (passions, sports, food, wellness). https://www.mastercard.com/mea/en/news-and-trends/press/2025/may/mastercard-economics-institute-what-moves-us-the-motivations-behind-global-travel-in-2025.html

**Implication:** story lenses ("architecture," "food history," "dark history," "film locations," "art," "for kids") are a cheap way to personalise and a natural premium feature.

## 10. Geographic shifts

- **US inbound fell 5.5% in 2025 while US outbound kept growing** (Q3 overseas departures +5.3%). Americans are going abroad, which favours Europe-first. https://mobi.htrends.com/?p=140416
- NYC: 12.5M international visitors in 2025 (−3.2%). https://skift.com/2026/03/24/new-york-city-tourism-ticks-up-but-international-visitors-lag/
- Japan: record 42.7M visitors in 2025. Kyoto had 12.68M foreign visitors (+17%). https://www.nippon.com/en/japan-data/h02829/kyoto-tourists-top-60-million-for-first-time-in-2025.html
- Italy is the #1 destination for US study-abroad students. https://www.iie.org/news/open-doors-2025-press-release/

**Implication:** Rome is supported by US outbound growth to Europe. Kyoto/Tokyo are high-growth Phase-2 cities, with local language, data and certification work required. NYC is a weaker launch city for *international* travellers in 2026–2027.

## Outlook 2027 (Research view, estimates)

1. "AI travel companion" becomes a recognised product category by late 2027, driven by Big Tech glasses advertising.
2. Travel eSIM bundling becomes standard in travel hardware and cards (expect more Pocketalk-style "data included" devices).
3. Destinations begin to *require or reward* digital visitor tools (timed entry, crowd routing), which opens B2B partnerships.
4. Trust becomes the differentiator for AI content, after high-profile hallucination stories. Sourced, verified content will command a premium.
